## Costa Smeralda at a Glance

The Costa Smeralda is Sardinia's ultra-luxury coast, where prime villas and sea-view apartments trade €6,000-15,000 per sqm and Porto Cervo marina frontage clears €10,000-18,000. Gross yields on trophy assets run 2-4%, which is the arithmetic of an asset bought to hold rather than to let. Treat Costa Smeralda as capital preservation with seasonal STR overlay rather than yield optimisation when foreign buyers allocate €2,000,000-8,000,000 capital bands on Gallura trophy stock reviewed with commercialista.

## Which Costa Smeralda segments work best for buyers?

A €4,000,000 Gallura budget buys very different assets depending on distance from the water. Inland Arzachena consorzio villas trade at €4,500-6,500 per sqm; Romazzino beach stock reaches €9,000-14,000. Where capital preservation is the goal rather than a beach frontage, the inland band is the better-value entry. Check the consorzio compliance history and any concession status, and get a geometra on site before assuming a renovation scope from drone photography.

| Pevero Golf ultra-prime | €12,000-18,000+ | €4M-15M+ | Dual golf and beach, jetty rights |

1. Default to Romazzino or Pevero when sea panorama and documented beach access justify trophy premium.
2. Use inland consorzio stock only when discount to sea comparables exceeds 25% with full compliance.
3. Reject waterfront marketing without concession contract attached to seller disclosure packet.

## What rental yields can Costa Smeralda property deliver?

Prime villas model at 2-4% gross on purchase price, and the net is considerably thinner. IMU alone runs €6,000-25,000 or more a year, management takes 20-30%, and rental income is taxed at 21% cedolare secca or 26% on most non-resident short-let arrangements. Personal use then competes with all of it. Regard peak weekly rents of €35,000-120,000 on five-to-eight-bedroom Pevero properties as impressive but seasonally concentrated when divided by €6-12 million acquisition costs.

|------------------|-----------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Sea-view 3-bed (Porto Rotondo) | €1.8-3M | €5,000-12,000 | 2.5-3.5% |
| Romazzino 4-bed villa with pool | €3.5-7M | €18,000-45,000 | 2-3.5% |
| Pevero Golf 5-bed villa | €5-10M+ | €25,000-70,000+ | 2-3% |
| Inland consorzio villa | €1.2-2.5M | €3,000-8,000 | 3-4% |

1. Model 10-14 rentable weeks unless luxury manager provides verified same-street history.
2. Deduct 25-30% management plus cleaning on full-service villa programmes before net yield.
3. Set against [Italy rental yield guide](/guides/italy-rental-yield-guide/) national benchmarks before Gallura allocation.

Long-term unfurnished leases are rare at the top end; when they exist, rents reflect Italian domestic affluence rather than international tourism peaks on identical trophy tickets marketed primarily for personal summer use.

## What do maritime concessions mean for coastal buyers?

Many coastal villas here depend on **concessioni demaniali marittime** — state licences for jetties, beach stairs and sea-level terraces. These sit outside the property deed entirely, carry fixed expiry dates and annual fees, and cannot be transferred without authority consent. A villa can be sold; its jetty licence may not follow. Approach concession uncertainty as deal-breaker risk because expired or non-transferable concessions can destroy waterfront value on €5,000,000+ Romazzino tickets marketed with private beach access claims without register extract attached.

| Check | Why it matters | Red flag |
|-------|----------------|----------|
| Register number + expiry | Transfer at sale | Expires within 5 years |
| Transfer rules | Automatic vs new application | Separate legal entity mooring |
| Annual fees | Carrying cost | Arrears from prior owner |
| Authorised plans | Structure compliance | Unauthorised sea-level works |
| AMP Tavolara rules | Protected zone limits | Agent summary slide only |

1. Commission maritime-law specialist review at €3,000-8,000 on any coastal trophy purchase.
2. Request concession contract itself, not broker summary, before waterfront premium payment.
3. Verify berth dimensions match vessel class when mooring bundled into villa marketing.

## What planning rules does the Consorzio Costa Smeralda enforce?

The Consorzio enforces architectural harmony across its member territory: roof pitch, exterior colours, stone and render materials, fence heights, native landscaping and even lighting spill are all controlled, with additional review for anything visible from the coast. Non-compliant works left by a previous owner — an enclosed loggia, a raised boundary wall — can require demolition or regularisation. A local geometra site visit before purchase is the only reliable way to establish which category a property falls into.

| Rule area | Practical impact | Timeline |
|-----------|------------------|----------|
| New build | Extremely limited | Value in existing fabric |
| Extensions | Comune + landscape + consorzio | 12-24 months |
| Non-compliant works | Fines + resale discount | Drone enforcement since 2024 |
| Material palette | Earth and pastel tones | Mandatory on exterior |
| Contingency budget | 20-35% on quoted build | Structural pinning common |

1. Commission geometra before offer on any property with pool terraces toward demanio zone.
2. Photograph every exterior element against approved plans during in-person inspection.
3. Budget 12-24 month permitting timeline for any permitted coastal renovation programme.

## How does summer STR peak season affect Costa Smeralda underwriting?

July and August generate 55-65% of annual gross rental revenue on a managed luxury villa here, with June and September extending the calendar at nightly rates 30-50% lower. The recurring spreadsheet error is double-counting: four to eight owner-use weeks must be reserved out of the rentable calendar, not added to it. Once they are, net yield on trophy Gallura stock falls well below what the peak-week rates suggest.

| Season | Revenue share | Rate signal |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| July-August | 55-65% annual gross | Peak pricing |
| June / September | Shoulder extension | 30-50% below peak |
| May / October | Niche yachting events | Thin demand |
| November-April | Minimal tourism rental | Carrying cost only |

1. Model 10-14 rentable weeks with written CIN feasibility from local avvocato before deposit.
2. Verify condominium regolamento on short stays before STR pro forma on resale listings.
3. Reserve 12-month cost reserve beyond purchase price for winter carrying costs without rental income.

## What should foreign buyers verify before Costa Smeralda rogito?

On €2,000,000-15,000,000 Gallura tickets, five items decide the deal: an extract from the maritime concession register where any beach, jetty or mooring right is claimed, the consorzio compliance history, cadastral floor-plan consistency with the building as it stands, CIN registration feasibility, and the IMU baseline. Instruct an avvocato with no connection to the selling agency, and have a geometra check every exterior element against the approved plans.

| Document | Why it matters | Red flag |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| Concession register | Waterfront value | Expiry within 5 years |
| Consorzio compliance | Resale and enforcement | Pending sanctions |
| Cadastral vs physical | Expanded villa stock | Layout mismatch |
| CIN feasibility | STR plan | Manager projection only |
| IMU / TARI baseline | Carrying cost | Agent omits annual stack |
| APE + seismic class | Masonry structures | Missing on pre-1980 fabric |

1. Commission maritime-law review on any coastal asset before waterfront premium payment.
2. Obtain written CIN path from local avvocato, not only rental manager revenue deck.
3. Cross-read [Sardinia guide](/guides/sardinia-property-investment-guide/) and [buy property Italy foreigner](/guides/buy-property-italy-foreigner/).
4. Budget 10-14% transaction costs including notary and registration tax with commercialista before wire.
5. Verify water supply contracts because many villas rely on cistern or shared borehole systems.
