## What Is Naples Property Investment in 2026?

Naples trades at €2,200-3,800 per sqm city-wide, with Vomero and Chiaia at €3,500-5,000, returning 4-5% gross furnished on well-bought hill stock. Entry runs 25-40% below Rome's prime districts, and the Amalfi Coast sits within reach — which is what draws American and British buyers to it.

Naples is Italy's third-largest city economy and operational hub for Campania tourism, port logistics, and southern Italy professional services. Foreign buyers historically overweighted Rome and Milan while Naples offered deeper yield on Gulf-view hillsides.

## Which Naples Districts Work Best for Foreign Buyers?

Four districts, four different propositions. Vomero balances yield and appreciation at €3,200-4,500 per sqm on €350,000-550,000 two-bedroom tickets. Chiaia is prestige at €3,800-5,500 for 3-4% gross. The UNESCO centro storico offers compliant short-stay at €2,500-4,200 plus renovation. Posillipo is trophy stock above €1,000,000.

- **Vomero:** Default foreign hill with funicular and metro, 4 to 5 percent gross furnished
- **Chiaia:** Prestige waterfront adjacency, capital preservation over yield
- **Centro storico:** UNESCO STR with Soprintendenza constraints
- **Posillipo:** Gulf-view trophy, thinner liquidity above 1 million EUR

## What Are Naples Price Bands and Rental Yields?

Naples spans a four-to-one range across five bands. Peripheral districts run €1,400-2,000 per sqm on €180,000-280,000 tickets; the renovated centro storico €2,500-4,200; Vomero two-bedrooms €3,200-4,500 on €350,000-550,000 stock; Chiaia €3,800-5,500. Furnished long leases in Vomero yield 4-5% gross against 4.5-6% on licensed short-let, where a CIN is in place and the building permits it.

| Zone | EUR per sqm | Typical ticket | Gross yield |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Peripheral | 1,400 to 2,000 | 180k to 280k EUR | 4.5 to 5.5% |
| Centro storico | 2,500 to 4,200 | 250k to 500k EUR | 4.5 to 6% STR |
| Vomero 2-bed | 3,200 to 4,500 | 350k to 550k EUR | 4 to 5% |
| Chiaia 2-bed | 3,800 to 5,500 | 450k to 750k EUR | 3 to 4% |

Winter short-stay occupancy without long-term anchor can collapse below 40 percent unless priced for business travel and cruise shoulder weeks.

## What Risks Should Naples Property Buyers Watch?

Six recurring problems. Seismic retrofit and conformità costs on pre-1970 buildings; Soprintendenza delays on centro façade work; short-stay regulation still evolving in the dense historic zones; missing parking deeds, which cap premium rents; abusi on enclosed terraces that only a geometric survey finds; and 9% registration tax on second homes.

- Seismic classification and required retrofit quotes on hill and centro stock
- Condominium regolamento on affitti brevi before short-stay underwriting
- Media risk perception slowing some foreign financing conversations
- Noise exposure on Spaccanapoli corridors for tourist rentals

None of these is a reason to avoid Naples. They are reasons to price the survey properly: a geometra report costing €800 to €1,500 routinely finds work worth ten times that on pre-1970 stock.

Six items before any deposit on a €350,000-550,000 Vomero ticket. Visura catastale and conformità on pre-1980 stock; seismic certificates; the regolamento position on short-stay; parking title in writing; the CIN registration path with the Comune di Napoli; and registration tax at 2% or 9% depending on the residency election.

## What Should Foreign Buyers Check on Naples Centro Stock?

Parking and conformità are the two findings that recur across Naples centro purchases. Foreign buyers frequently discover that a Gulf-view apartment carries non-assignable parking and a built layout that does not match the cadastral plan, both of which transfer with the flat at the rogito.

| Check | Typical exposure |
|---|---|
| Non-assignable parking | €12,000-€28,000 a year to replace |
| Conformità edilizia | Gap transfers to the buyer |
| Straordinaria programmes | Read the assemblea minutes before the caparra |
| IMU (second home) | 0.86%-1.06% of cadastral value |

- Request 3 years of administrator spese history before committing; a pending works vote comes with the flat.
- Vomero and the historic centre behave as different markets; confirm which one the address is in.
- Short-let use requires CIN registration and a condominium regolamento that permits it.
