## What Is Turin Property Investment in 2026?

Turin property investment typically means Piedmont capital tickets at 1,600 to 3,000 EUR per sqm city-wide with Crocetta and San Salvario at 2,400 to 3,200 EUR per sqm, 4 to 5 percent gross furnished yields versus Milan at 3 to 4 percent, 40 to 50 percent lower entry per sqm than Milan, and foreign buyers from France and Switzerland targeting Politecnico tenant depth and Alps weekend access in northwest Italy shortlists.

Turin is Italy's fourth-largest city economy, anchored by automotive heritage, design institutes, and remote-worker cafe culture that foreign media underweights relative to Milan hype. Entry per sqm remains materially below Milan while governance, banking infrastructure, and high-speed rail links stay northern-Italian standard.

## Which Turin Districts Work Best for Foreign Buyers?

Turin district selection typically means Crocetta and Cit Turin for elegant stock at 2,600 to 3,500 EUR per sqm with 4 to 5 percent gross yields, San Salvario for student demand at 2,200 to 2,900 EUR per sqm with 5 to 6 percent on micro-units, Quadrilatero Romano for boutique short-stay, and Lingotto regeneration at lower basis with construction noise through 2030 that map when foreign buyers compare [Turin vs Milan](/compare/turin-vs-milan-property/) on identical 350,000 EUR budget bands.

- **Crocetta / Cit Turin:** 2,600 to 3,500 EUR per sqm, 320,000 to 520,000 EUR two-bed tickets, 4 to 5 percent gross
- **San Salvario:** 2,200 to 2,900 EUR per sqm, student and nightlife tenant mix, 5 to 6 percent on micro-units
- **Quadrilatero Romano:** boutique short-stay near centro, smaller inventory
- **Lingotto south:** regeneration upside, compare [Maciachini](/projects/maciachini-urban-retreat/) northwest tickets

### Crocetta and Cit Turin

Elegant boulevards, museums, and consistent elevator stock in renovated condominiums. Default district for professionals and French-speaking buyers who want Parisian grid feel at Italian prices. Two-bedroom tickets often run 320,000 to 520,000 EUR.

### San Salvario

Multicultural restaurant scene and Politecnico inflows create furnished and short-stay demand. Noise and nightlife require tenant screening discipline. Gross yields on student housing can reach 5 to 6 percent on well-bought micro-units.

### Lingotto and south regeneration

Former Fiat Lingotto factory district now mixes retail, offices, and residential infill. Regeneration upside with construction noise during hold period. Compare against Milan [Maciachini Urban Retreat](/projects/maciachini-urban-retreat/) off-plan at higher northwest tickets.

## What Do Turin Condominium Charges Actually Cover?

Building age drives the carry in Turin. Foreign buyers acquiring Crocetta and centro stock are typically buying 1960s or pre-1980 blocks where lift modernisation, façade programmes and heating conversions arrive as extraordinary assessments voted by the assembly rather than as predictable annual charges.

| Line | Band |
|---|---|
| Spese condominiali | €1,200-€2,400 a year |
| Lift modernisation | €8,000-€15,000 on 1960s blocks |
| IMU (second home) | 0.86%-1.06% of cadastral value |
| Cedolare secca (long lease) | 21% of gross rent |

- Request 3 years of administrator spese history and the assemblea minutes before the caparra.
- A straordinaria vote already passed transfers to the buyer, so read the minutes rather than the listing.
- Turin demand is salaried and academic, which suits 12-month furnished leases over short lets.
