## Foreign Buyer Practicalities

Foreign buyer practicalities in Sorrento turn on landscape vincolo, condominium permission for tourist use, and cadastral plan gaps on historic palazzi. On tickets below €900,000 any one of the three can block the intended use after purchase, so all three belong in the pre-compromesso file rather than the post-rogito one.
