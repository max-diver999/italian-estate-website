## What heritage and Soprintendenza rules apply?

Most Municipio I buildings fall under Soprintendenza Speciale jurisdiction, which means façade, roof, window and structural work requires authorisation. Budget 4-9 months for the permission and a 25-45% cost premium over identical work on unrestricted suburban stock. Interior upgrades planned around a letting season rarely survive contact with that timetable.

## What STR rules apply in Rome centro storico?

Letting under 30 days requires a national CIN, a regional CIR, Alloggiati Web guest filing, the fire-safety kit under DL 145/2023, and — the one that catches buyers out — written condominium tolerance. Many Municipio I assemblies banned tourist use outright after resident complaints, and that ban survives a change of owner.

## Foreign Buyer Practicalities

Centro storico purchases fail on inherited abusi edilizi and condominium short-let bans far more often than on notary mechanics, and both surface late on €450,000-€1.2M tickets. Budget €8,000-€15,000 of due diligence on a €700,000 acquisition and settle each in writing before the caparra.

- An abuso transfers with the building, not with whoever created it, so a mezzanine or closed loggia added decades ago becomes your problem at resale
- A building can sit in a zone permitting tourist use while its own regolamento forbids it, which invalidates the yield model the purchase was based on
- Both answers belong in the pre-compromesso file; neither is fixable after the rogito
