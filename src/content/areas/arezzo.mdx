## Overview

Arezzo is the capoluogo of eastern Tuscany, sitting directly on the A1 between Florence and Rome with a fast-train station on the same line. Its economy runs on a hospital, a university campus and a goldsmithing sector rather than on tourism, though the monthly Antiques Fair produces genuine short-let spikes.

Entry tickets land 40-70% below Florence centro, and the resale bid comes mainly from Florentine and Roman upgraders rather than from international buyers.

| Factor | Arezzo signal | Foreign buyer note |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| Tenant depth | Hospital + university | Year-round vs event STR |
| vs Florence | ~€4,737/m² avg | Lower entry, thinner foreign pool |
| vs Siena | ~€3,200 centro | Less Palio branding |
| Resale anchor | Three closed sales | Same micro-district |

1. Hospital fellowship and university intake renew each September when parking deeds attach.
2. Antiques Fair weeks lift licensed STR 30-40% above winter baselines on centro stock.
3. Foreign enquiry stays thinner than Florence but EU yield landlords target furnished leases.

## Rental Yields and Tenant Profiles

Furnished long-term leases in central Arezzo to hospital staff and university tenants generally deliver 4.5-5.5% gross on tickets under €250,000, and the tenant pool is stable enough that remote signing from an EU employer is routine. A deeded parking space is the variable that most often decides whether a listing lets at all.

Running the same €220,000 bilocale as a licensed Antiques Fair short-let instead moves the net figure by 150-200 basis points once IMU and cedolare secca at 21% are applied.

| Strategy | Gross yield | Tenant type |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Centro furnished long lease | 4-5.5% | Hospital, university |
| Periphery value renovation | 5-6% with risk | Local families |
| Licensed centro STR | 4-5% peak weeks | Antiques Fair, events |
| Trophy centro hold | 2.5-3.5% | Lifestyle only |

A €220,000 bilocale at €900 monthly generates €10,800 annual gross equal to 4.9% before IMU and cedolare secca. September university intake and hospital fellowship calendars support renewal pricing when furnishing includes utility-inclusive packages and parking deed attachments documented in marketing materials before remote tenant signing from northern European employers.

Set yield math against [Siena](/areas/siena/) centro tickets at higher per sqm basis and [Florence](/areas/florence/) corporate lease profiles compressing toward 3-4% gross on premium acquisition basis despite higher absolute rents on northwest corporate housing demand.

## Transportation and Regional Access

The A1 puts Florence 70-90 minutes away by car and Rome 90-120, while regional rail reaches Santa Maria Novella in 60-75 minutes. That combination is what makes Arezzo work as a split-week base: close enough to two office cities to commute occasionally, far enough to cost a third of either.

Treat Peretola airport at 80-100 minutes and Val d'Orcia approaches at 45-60 minutes as dual-track allocation options within single province budgets.

## What works best in Arezzo centro versus Giotto corridors?

Two strategies, two different parts of town. Piazza Grande and the medieval wall-ring host the Antiques Fair and the Giostra del Saracino, lifting licensed short lets 30-40% above winter baselines. The Giotto and XXV Aprile corridors hold 1970s-1990s condominiums with parking, which is what hospital furnished leases require.

Flag upper-floor palazzo units without garage deeds as tenant rejection risk for foreign landlords targeting twelve-month hospital contracts.

| District | €/m² band | Best strategy | Parking |
|----------|-----------|---------------|---------|
| Centro Città | ~€2,382 | STR + events | Scarce |
| Giotto / XXV Aprile | ~€2,300 | Long-term lease | Underground common |
| Piero della Francesca | ~€2,150 | Family lease | Mixed |
| Palazzo del Pero | ~€1,041 | Renovation value | Car required |

1. Verify CIN transferability before Antiques Fair STR pro forma on centro tickets.
2. Request elevator certificate and three-year spese on pre-1990 Giotto towers.
3. Reject third-floor walk-ups without lift conformity for hospital tenant pipelines.

## Municipal Taxes and Ownership Costs

IMU on an Arezzo second home is calculated from the cadastral rendita and the commune's multiplier, which means a centro palazzo in category A/2 or A/1 carries a materially heavier annual charge than a periphery condominium bought for the same €250,000. Budget 10-15% of price for closing costs, including the 9% registration tax that applies to second-home purchases from private sellers, and add cedolare secca at 21% on furnished lease gross.

| Cost line | Typical band | Note |
|-----------|--------------|------|
| Registration tax | 9% non-primary | Cadastral value basis |
| Closing all-in | 10-15% | Notary + agency |
| Cedolare secca LT | 21% | Furnished lease gross |
| Cedolare secca STR | 26% | Beyond two-property threshold |
| IMU | Varies by category | Request visura pre-offer |

1. Request updated visura catastale before modeling net yield on portal gross rent.
2. Model cedolare secca path with commercialista before first wire to notaio escrow.
3. Benchmark against IMU bands between centro palazzo and periphery condominium before offer.

## What risks should foreign buyers watch in Arezzo?

The pros and cons of Arezzo divide cleanly: a third of Florence's per sqm price and a non-touristic tenant base on the plus side, a shallower resale market and no international brand premium on the minus. Three issues recur in underwriting. Student turnover near the university corridors produces shorter tenancies and more re-letting cost than the headline yield suggests. Parking in the walled centro is scarce, and a flat without a garage deed rents to a narrower pool. And peripheral stock priced 8-12% above the OMI quartiere band resells slowly, because the international buyer who absorbs mispricing in Florence does not shop here. Pull elevator conformity certificates and three years of spese before the compromesso.

| Risk | Typical trigger | Mitigation |
|------|-----------------|------------|
| Student turnover | September lease gaps | Utility-inclusive twelve-month contracts |
| Parking scarcity | Centro palazzo | Garage deed in lease annex |
| Thin foreign resale | Periphery mispricing | Three closed sales same micro-district |
| Elevator compliance | 1960s-1980s towers | Lift certificate before marketing |
| STR regulation | CIN transfer gaps | Verify municipal registration |

1. Attach administrator spese history before compromesso on pre-1980 condominiums.
2. Model vacancy separately for academic calendar gaps on student-heavy corridors.
3. Stress-test 3-5% agent commission plus IMU throughout hold before periphery plays.

A five-year yield hold works best in the Giotto corridor near the hospital, where lease depth is real and glamour is low; a lifestyle-primary hold justifies the centro premium; a short-let play depends entirely on Antiques Fair weekends and needs a CIN.

Stress-test currency, tax residency and whether the eventual buyer will be Florentine or Roman before committing the same €250,000 to any of the three.

## What should foreign buyers verify before Arezzo rogito?

On €220,000-280,000 inland Tuscan stock, four documents decide the deal: the elevator conformity certificate in any multi-storey palazzo, three years of administrator spese with approved extraordinary works, written confirmation that the parking deed transfers, and a visura showing the cadastral category matches actual use. Check CIN transferability if short-let is planned, and confirm the IMU band before authorising a wire from a non-resident account.

| Document | Why it matters | Red flag |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| Elevator certificate | Hospital tenant rejection | Missing on pre-1980 towers |
| Spese history (3 yr) | Net yield after condo fees | Agent omits from packet |
| Parking deed | Tenant conversion | Shared courtyard only |
| CIN registration | Antiques Fair STR | STR pro forma without transfer |
| Cadastral visura | IMU and registration tax | Layout mismatch |

1. Request elevator certificate and three-year condominium spese history before compromesso.
2. Cross-read [Tuscany inland guide](/guides/tuscany-inland-property-guide/) and [Arezzo vs Siena](/compare/arezzo-vs-siena-property/).
3. A commercialista should model IMU and the 21% cedolare secca before any compromesso wire leaves.
