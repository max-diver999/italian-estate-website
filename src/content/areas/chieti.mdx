## Hill Town Practicalities and Renovation

Exterior facade and roof work in the hill centro typically needs municipal heritage consultation costing €2,000-5,000 with 6-10 week timelines before a contractor mobilises — a step that listings illustrated with cathedral photography rarely mention. Two other issues surface repeatedly on pre-1980 stock: conformità gaps on later annexes, and shared stairwells with no lift, which students and their families reject outright above the second floor.

| Renovation type | Typical cost | Timeline | Red flag |
|-----------------|--------------|----------|----------|
| Facade heritage review | €2,000-5,000 | 6-10 weeks | Visible centro work |
| Interior MEP | €40,000-80,000 | 3-6 months | Cadastral mismatch |
| Roof remediation | €15,000-35,000 | 2-4 months | Hill exposure |
| Elevator retrofit | €25,000-50,000 | 4-8 months | Pre-1970 towers |

1. Audit conformità on planned interior capex before contractor mobilization on hill stock.
2. Reject upper-floor palazzo units without parking deed for campus tenant marketing.
3. Commission geometra filing before MEP budgets lock at compromesso stage.
