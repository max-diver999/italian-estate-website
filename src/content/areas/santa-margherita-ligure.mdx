## Santa Margherita Ligure at a Glance

Santa Margherita Ligure is the working town of the Tigullio Gulf: a real harbour, a year-round population, and coastal apartments at €4,000-8,000 per sqm, with promenade positions reaching €10,000. ### Harbour promenade and marina front

Harbour-facing apartments command €6,500-10,000 per sqm on terraces with yacht basin sightlines. STR operators target April-October peaks with licensed inventory and professional management handling marina-event weekends.

### Sea-view terraces and Corte lanes

Hillside lanes offer character apartments at €4,000-5,500 per sqm with geotechnical diligence requirements and landscape vincolo on terrace expansions. Visit properties during rain season to assess drainage before capex budgets lock.

### Rapallo border spillover

Eastern border zones toward Rapallo deliver value at €3,500-4,800 per sqm with five-minute rail access to Santa Margherita services, suited to yield investors accepting slightly reduced prestige branding.

## How does yacht marina economics shape Santa Margherita yields?

The marina generates a tenant class most Riviera towns lack: crew managers, charter brokers and seasonal yacht staff who take furnished, utility-inclusive lets and renew annually where parking and marina walkability are documented. On a €650,000 harbour ticket that produces a stable twelve-month income. Treat the Portofino ferry traffic and regatta weekends as supplementary short-let income rather than annualising event pricing, which is volatile by nature.
