The second constraint is parking: wall-ring streets have almost none, which pushes tenants toward garages in Santa Maria degli Angeli and makes a documented parking annexe part of the lease rather than an extra.

Heritage protection governs what foreign buyers can change inside the UNESCO walls. Facades, openings and roof structures on Assisi centro stock require clearance before works, so the approval timetable rather than a contractor's quote sets the realistic date on which a restoration can actually begin.

| Element | Constraint |

| Roof structures | Clearance required before works |
