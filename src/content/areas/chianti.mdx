## Heritage, Agricultural, and Planning Rules

Chianti heritage and planning rules typically mean vincolo paesaggistico landscape protection requiring Soprintendenza sign-off on exterior work, Chianti DOCG production caps inheriting with vineyard land, and agricultural conversion requiring 4-8 month municipal applications that MORE Group reviews before foreign buyers commit pool budgets on casali marketed with terrace views alone without landscape authority pre-clearance.

- **Landscape vincolo:** Exterior windows, doors, and roof materials need Soprintendenza approval in 60-90 days
- **Heritage consultancy:** Budget €2,000-5,000 for specialist filings on listed casali
- **DOCG vineyard land:** Production caps and grape variety rules transfer with deed
- **Change-of-use:** Agricultural to residential or hospitality costs €5,000-15,000 in consultancy
- **Interior work:** Modernization usually permitted without heritage delay when exterior unchanged

Request detailed uso del suolo land-use certificate before purchasing any Chianti property with agricultural component.

- **Agricultural land:** Change-of-use process costs €5,000-15,000 with 4-8 month timelines
- **Seller documentation:** CIN and agriturismo licences must match exact cadastral parcel
- **Restoration overrun:** Budget 15-20% contingency above geometra quotes on raw casali
