## What Should Foreign Buyers Know About UNESCO Renovation and STR Rules?

Exterior work in the Sassi requires Soprintendenza approval, with heritage consultancy at €3,000-8,000 and typical timelines of 8-12 weeks, and CIN registration must be in place for any short-let. The specific Matera risk sits inside the rock: pre-1980 cave conversions frequently lack conformità, and humidity management drives mechanical and electrical budgets that a surface inspection will not reveal. Settle both before fixing an interior budget at compromesso.

| Requirement | Timeline / cost | Foreign buyer action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soprintendenza exterior | 8-12 weeks | Budget before compromesso |
| Cave humidity remediation | €15,000-35,000 | Geometra survey mandatory |
| CIN registration | At rogito | Verify transferability |
| SCIA / occupancy | Pre-marketing | Avvocato review |
| Long-lease fallback | Year-round | Reduce Nov-Mar void |

Licensed STR in Matera requires administrator statements documenting spese history on shared cave stairwell stock. Blend STR supplements with long-lease professional tenants to reduce November-March void risk when festival pro forma omits hospital employment depth.

## What Is Specific About Owning Sassi Stock?

Cave dwellings carry maintenance obligations that ordinary apartment stock does not. Foreign buyers in the Sassi are acquiring UNESCO-protected fabric where humidity management, structural monitoring and heritage clearance for any alteration are continuing costs, on top of IMU at 0.86-1.06% of cadastral value each year.

| Item | Why it recurs |
|---|---|
| Humidity management | Inherent to rock-cut structures |
| Structural monitoring | Ongoing, not a single survey |
| Heritage clearance | Required for visible alteration |
| IMU (second home) | 0.86%-1.06% of cadastral value |

- Sassi stock cannot be replicated, so value rests on tourism demand and what the heritage authority permits.
- Confirm the heritage position on any planned alteration before the caparra, not after.
- Short-let use additionally requires CIN registration and the comune's approval.
