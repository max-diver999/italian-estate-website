## What Is Cisternino Property Investment?

Cisternino is the Valle d'Itria's value entry: centro storico stock from about €150,000 and countryside trulli from €120,000, roughly 15-25% below the equivalent Ostuni ticket. Licensed pool villas return 5-6% gross with 60-70% occupancy from May to October. The trade is village scale in place of white-city recognition.

Cisternino ranks among Italy's most beautiful villages with whitewashed centro lanes, famous fornelli butcher restaurants, and UNESCO landscape proximity. International enquiry growth trails Ostuni but accelerates as buyers discover lower entry pricing with similar Adriatic beach access within 20-30 minutes via Fasano and Torre Canne corridors each spring Gate-away listing season.

## What legal checks does a Cisternino purchase need?

The purchase itself is the standard Italian sequence: codice fiscale, notary rogito in 60-90 days, no restriction for EU citizens and reciprocity for others. What is specific here is land: agricultural parcels need cadastral verification and confirmation of the buildable envelope under UNESCO landscape rules before any deposit is wired on a contrada plot.

- **EU citizens:** Standard rogito within 60-90 days with codice fiscale and notaio escrow
- **Non-EU buyers:** Reciprocity-country clearance plus independent avvocato review required
- **Agricultural land:** Cadastral verification and UNESCO buildable envelope before deposit
- **Heritage centro:** Listing obligations may restrict exterior modifications on pre-1970 stock
- **Transaction stack:** Reserve 10-12% atop headline price for registration and notary costs

Independent avvocato should review seller declarations, mortgage encumbrances, and pending commune enforcement actions before deposit transfer on multi-heir village inheritance sales common in contrada lanes where shared wall obligations govern winter maintenance contracts between neighbors on medieval stone fabric.
