## Quick Answer

Bellagio sits on the promontory where Lake Como splits into its two southern arms, and that geography caps supply: prime promenade apartments trade at €6,000-15,000 per sqm, roughly double Como city's €3,500-6,500 band. Licensed short-let on a lake-view two-bedroom returns 2-3% gross on tickets above €800,000, so the case here is capital preservation with income as a secondary benefit. Buy the bilocale with a registered parking deed attached before signing the compromesso.

## Overview

Three forces set Bellagio pricing: a promontory with almost no developable land, a century of international name recognition, and a conservation regime that blocks new volume on the waterfront. Together they lift per sqm values far above the Lombardy average and keep the buyer pool international — American, British, German and Swiss purchasers dominate the €800,000-3,000,000 band.

Owners who treat the address as a store of value rather than a yield instrument tend to be satisfied with the outcome.

| Factor | Bellagio signal | Foreign buyer note |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Brand position | Pearl of Lake Como | Global recognition premium |
| Supply | Very limited | Terrain and heritage caps |
| Tenant depth | Seasonal tourism | No university anchor |
| Resale anchor | Harbour-view stock | 12-24 months priced right |

1. Tourism economy runs May through September without year-round employment anchor.
2. Inventory elasticity is near zero because new building faces landscape vincoli.
3. Resale depth depends on global HNW cycles more than Italian mortgage buyers.

## Rental Yields and Tenant Profiles

A licensed lake-view two-bedroom in Bellagio typically prints 2-3% gross on a €900,000-plus ticket. July and August fill at 70-85%, but shoulder months realistically model at 30-40% and the winter calendar is close to dead. Net cash flow moves 150-200 basis points between two identical €1,100,000 purchases depending on IMU band, whether the owner elects cedolare secca at 21%, and whether management costs 15% or 25% of gross.

| Centro 1-bed no view | 1.5-2.2% | Budget STR |
| Villa pool dock | 1.8-3.0% | Luxury weekly lets |
| Hillside hybrid | 2.2-3.1% | Owner use plus STR |

1. Model net yield after IMU and cedolare secca at 21% before marketing peak-season STR.
2. Assume 40-55% annual occupancy unless verified booking data exists for exact address.
3. Set yield math against [Como city](/areas/como/) at 3-4.5% gross on lower entry tickets.

## What risks should foreign buyers watch in Bellagio?

Three risks dominate Bellagio underwriting. Winter voids can consume more than 50% of the calendar, which is why gross yields sit at 2-3% rather than the 5-6% common in Lombardy's cities. Promenade addresses carry ferry and traffic noise that shows up in guest reviews but rarely in listing photos. And secondary interior stock priced above closed comparables can take 18-36 months to resell. Confirm the CIN registration path and pull three years of administrator spese before the compromesso.

| Risk | Typical trigger | Mitigation |
|------|-----------------|------------|
| Seasonal void | Nov-Mar occupancy | Model zero-revenue months |
| Thin resale | Interior no-view stock | Buy harbour sightline |
| Dock encumbrance | Demanio lacuale gaps | Independent concession review |
| Renovation delay | Soprintendenza queue | 12-18 month permit budget |
| STR regulation | Condominium bans | Verify regolamento before deposit |

1. Reject STR pro forma without verified CIN path and condominium tourist-use approval.
2. Attach lakefront concession documentation before marketing dock access to yacht tenants.
3. Model vacancy separately for November-March on identical €1,000,000 underwriting sheets.

## How strong is resale liquidity in Bellagio promenade stock?

Harbour-view Bellagio units priced against three closed comparables and sold with a transferable parking deed generally clear in 12-24 months. Interior stock priced off spring portal asking figures can sit for 24-36 months, because the foreign enquiry pool that sustains the promenade thins quickly once the lake view goes.

Sellers planning a seven-year exit should model Bellagio, Como city and the mid-lake villages of Menaggio and Varenna on identical €900,000-1,500,000 assumptions.

| Segment | Typical marketing period | Buyer pool |
|---------|-------------------------|------------|
| Promenade view + parking | 12-24 months priced right | US/UK/Swiss HNW |
| Lakefront villa trophy | 12-18 months off-market | Global yachting families |
| Hillside view terrace | 18-30 months | Lifestyle hybrid owners |
| Secondary interior | 24-36 months | Value domestic buyers |

1. Price to three winter closed sales before spring portal listing peaks.
2. Market parking deeds explicitly in English-language listing packs for American enquiry share near 45%.
3. Stress-test 3-5% agent commission plus IMU paid throughout hold before interior value plays.

Resale liquidity on Lake Como behaves unlike any Italian city market, and the difference is worth stating plainly before a purchase rather than discovering it at sale. The buyer pool is small, international and specific: Swiss, German, American and British purchasers who want this village rather than a lake house generally. That produces a market where a correctly priced harbour-view apartment with a transferable parking deed clears in 12 to 24 months, and an interior unit priced off spring asking figures can sit for 24 to 36. Neither timeline is a distress signal; both are normal for scarcity assets sold to a narrow pool. The practical consequence is that a €900,000-1,500,000 exit needs to be planned across a selling season rather than a quarter, and modelled with IMU and spese running throughout, which typically removes a full year of gross yield from the return.

## What should foreign buyers verify before Bellagio rogito?

Four checks matter before wiring on Lake Como stock at €900,000-2,500,000: lakefront concession rights where a jetty, boathouse or mooring is included; three years of condominium spese and any approved extraordinary works; whether the parking deed actually transfers with the apartment; and whether the cadastral category matches the use.

Confirm CIN transferability if short-let is part of the plan, and check the IMU cadastral band, since non-resident owners pay the full second-home rate.

| Document | Why it matters | Red flag |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| Lake concession | Dock and beach rights | Marketing without title |
| Spese history (3 yr) | Net yield after €250/mo condo | Agent omits from packet |
| Parking deed | Anglo buyer conversion | Shared courtyard only |
| Cadastral plan | Room count vs registry | Terrace not registered |
| CIN registration | STR compliance | Pro forma without transfer |

1. Request concession certificate and three-year condominium spese history before compromesso.
2. Confirm parking deed for STR marketing in listing annexes attached to booking platforms.
3. Cross-read [Lake Como guide](/guides/lake-como-property-investment-guide/) and [due diligence Italy](/guides/due-diligence-italy-property/).
4. Put IMU and cedolare secca at 21% into the model with a commercialista ahead of the compromesso.
5. Order independent avvocato review on seller declarations and landscape vincoli on terrace works.
6. Verify CIN transferability before peak-season STR pro forma on promenade tickets below €1,500,000.
