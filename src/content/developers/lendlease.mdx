Lendlease off-plan contracts typically include bank-guaranteed milestone payments tied to foundation completion, structural closure, facade installation, and MEP commissioning verified by independent engineers. Foreign buyers should budget 8-12% transaction stack including VAT treatment on new-build reviewed with commercialista, notary fees, and registration costs distinct from resale palazzo purchases at 9% registration tax bands.

Lendlease global portfolio includes mixed-use and infrastructure delivery beyond Milan residential, signaling construction continuity across phases when local Italian promoters might stall on financing gaps mid-build.

Institutional milestone practice is the main thing separating this stock from a boutique off-plan purchase, and it is worth understanding rather than simply trusting. Payments are released against certified stages of construction rather than against calendar dates, each stage backed by a bank guarantee, with independent engineer inspection rights written into the schedule. That structure protects a buyer in two ways: capital is not exposed ahead of physical progress, and the guarantee sits with a bank rather than with the developer's own balance sheet. Two questions still belong in the file. Which bank issues the guarantee, and what is its standing — a guarantee is only as good as its issuer. And does the guarantee cover the full amount paid to date, or a capped portion of it? On a €380,000-520,000 unit completing in Q2 2028, those two answers decide what a delay actually costs you.
