## Who is D'Amico Gruppo and what does it build?

Gruppo Invest D'Amico is a Brindisi-province group that markets and coordinates countryside villa builds around Ostuni. The output is large-plot, low-volume product rather than standardised units: the flagship Contrada Rascina build pairs roughly 300 sqm of volume with 14,788 sqm of land, and foreign buyers are quoted on application at that tier.

Gruppo Invest D'Amico occupies the mid-to-upper Puglia villa segment where plot size and construction flexibility matter more than standardized apartment towers. Ostuni's white-city tourism brand supports premium countryside villas with pools, while Brindisi airport connectivity (roughly 45 minutes) keeps the location accessible for international owners.

## What does the Rascina villa construction project offer?

The [Rascina listing](/projects/ostuni-rascina-villa-construction/) is the clearest single asset in the D'Amico portfolio: approximately 300 sqm of built volume, a swimming pool, and 14,788 sqm of countryside land in Contrada Rascina. That parcel is nearly double the 8,000 sqm typical of an Ostuni villa plot, and the land is what foreign buyers are paying the premium for.

Land at that scale changes the planning question. Olive cultivation, a second accessory building and agriturismo use all become arguable on 14,788 sqm where they would not be on 8,000 sqm, but each of those routes is a separate municipal filing rather than something the villa permit carries with it.

Construction-phase buyers may capture appreciation as build progresses, but they accept completion risk, contractor coordination exposure, and permit verification obligations that turnkey promoters absorb.

## Does D'Amico sell as principal or as agent?

Seller role on a D'Amico listing is not constant, and it moves the tax bill as much as the risk. A sale by the construction company is a VAT transaction at 10% on new-build stock, while a private resale is taxed at 9% registration on the cadastral value. Establish which applies before any deposit leaves.

Marketing materials present villas across several Ostuni contrade under whichever role the seller holds on that parcel. Before a reservation deposit:

- Confirm whether D'Amico sells as developer principal or licensed agent
- Identify title holder on land registry extract
- Verify building permit attaches to correct cadastral parcel
- Review milestone schedule if under construction

Buyers from abroad should engage avvocato in Brindisi or Bari familiar with agricultural land conversions common in Ostuni countryside sales.

## What rental strategy fits a large Ostuni villa?

Large villas with pools near Ostuni often achieve €120-180 nightly in July-August with 60-75% peak occupancy under professional management. Multi-bedroom layouts suit family groups booking week-long stays. The strategy that works on this format is a short, dense summer season rather than year-round occupancy: demand outside May to September falls away sharply.

Long-term winter leases run lower (€800-1,500 monthly) but reduce management intensity. Blended gross yields of 5-7% appear on €500k-800k tickets when STR season performs; net yields fall after management, cleaning, and cedolare secca at 21% or 26% on gross rent.

## How does the purchase process work on a D'Amico listing?

The process on a price-on-application asset starts earlier than on fixed-price stock, because the quote itself has to be assembled before anything can be modelled. Expect the sequence below to run 4 to 8 months from first enquiry to rogito, and longer where the villa is still under construction and payments are tied to build stages.

1. Request POA quote with cadastral map, permit copies, and milestone schedule
2. Engage independent avvocato for land class and abusi clearance
3. Inspect construction progress physically if under build
4. Structure compromesso with delay penalties and completion guarantees
5. Rogito with habitability certificate and geometric survey

## What due diligence does a D'Amico Gruppo purchase require?

Due diligence here is dominated by two items a city-apartment checklist does not cover: the agricultural classification of the parcel, and the permit trail behind the pool and any accessory buildings. On a 14,788 sqm contrada plot those two decide what foreign buyers may legally build, extend and let.

- Verify seller role: developer principal vs agent mandate
- Confirm building permit and SCIA or equivalent documentation
- Review land registry for agricultural vs residential classification
- Inspect pool compliance setbacks on large plots
- Model IMU on rural property categories
- Compare POA €/sqm against [Ostuni Domus €470k](/projects/ostuni-new-villa-pool-470k/) turnkey benchmark

## What should be verified before closing?

Before compromesso on D'Amico Gruppo listings, verify seller role, land scale compliance, and construction progress in writing.

**Principal vs agent.** D'Amico may market as developer coordinator or licensed agent. Title holder on visura catastale must match compromesso counterparty. Agency mandates require explicit commission disclosure.

**Large-plot compliance.** Rascina-scale 14,788 sqm parcels need pool setback verification, access road maintenance obligations, and agricultural land class review. Agriturismo ambitions require separate municipal planning paths.

**Construction milestones.** Under-build stock needs photographed progress, permit validity dates, and contractor identity before milestone payments. Snagging lists on partial completion reduce rogito disputes.

**Rural utilities.** Confirm mains water, sewage, and electricity capacity for 300 sqm built volume. Autoclave and septic designs must meet habitability standards at final inspection.

**Tax and rental.** Model IMU on large rural plots and STR cedolare secca at 21% or 26% on gross rent. Wire purchase funds only to notaio conto provvisorio after avvocato review.

Villas in the Ostuni countryside frequently sit on land classified as agricultural, and that classification governs what may be built or extended. Engaging an avvocato in Brindisi or Bari who handles these conversions routinely is worth more here than any negotiation on price.
