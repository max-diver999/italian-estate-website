## Promoter vs Developer Contract Clarity

Albero Architecture marketing on Prandina sales packs does not automatically make Albero the legal developer on compromesso documents. OKAM Italy typically appears as developer brand while FRIMM executes construction and Albero facilitates promotion channels toward international buyer databases familiar with Navigli lifestyle keywords.

Foreign buyers wiring reservation deposits should match beneficiary account names against entities listed in compromesso signature blocks reviewed by avvocato before any transfer, because promoter branding on PDF exposés sometimes differs from escrow account holders on milestone schedules.

Promoter and developer are separate legal positions in Italy, and a foreign buyer who conflates them is relying on a guarantee that does not exist. A promoter markets the scheme, produces the brochure, runs the enquiry desk and often appears on the signage; a developer holds the permesso di costruire, signs the construction contract and carries the completion obligation. Only the second is a party to your compromesso. The test is mechanical rather than legal: ask for the draft compromesso, read the vendor line, and check that company against a visura camerale for its capital and its filed accounts. On a €450,000-650,000 Martesana unit, an 18 month delay costs real money, and the penalty clause binds the named vendor alone. Albero's involvement on Prandina is a marketing signal about product quality: it is not a balance sheet standing behind your handover date.
