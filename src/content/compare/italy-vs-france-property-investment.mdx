## How does the French IFI wealth tax compare with Italian IMU?

Tax architecture means France applies IFI wealth tax from €1.3 million net real-estate wealth at 0.5-1.5% sliding rates, while Italy charges IMU at 0.86-1.06% cadastral value plus IVAFE at 0.76% for non-residents on property holdings. Buyer scenario modeling shows Italy typically lighter on sub-€2M portfolios, whereas French IFI exceeds Italian combined charges above €3M net real-estate.









1. Model IFI from €1.3M net real-estate wealth before expanding French Riviera exposure.
2. Compare IMU plus IVAFE stacks on Italian tickets below €2M total portfolio value.
3. Run cross-border adviser models when rental income exceeds €40,000 gross per unit annually.

| Tax item | France | Italy |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Wealth tax trigger | €1.3M net RE | No net-wealth tax on RE |
| Annual property charge | Taxe foncière + IFI | IMU 0.86-1.06% |
| Non-resident surcharge | Social charges on rent | IVAFE 0.76% on property value |
| Rental income option | Progressive up to 45% | Cedolare secca 21% flat |
| Purchase tax (second home) | 5-6% droits de mutation | 9% registration tax |

Always model with a cross-border tax adviser. Treaty relief, domicile elections, and company structures change outcomes materially.
