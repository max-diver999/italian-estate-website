## How do Turin vs Milan compare at a glance?

North-west Italy offers a clean trade. Turin references near €2,200 per sqm and returns 4-5% gross furnished; Milan averages closer to €5,750 per sqm and returns 3-4% on tickets above €500,000. One buys cash flow, the other buys corporate tenant quality and a deeper exit. Treat 50-70 minute.

| Foreign liquidity | Medium | Very high |

Turin's Politecnico corridors deliver 4.5-5% gross on €280,000 tickets at around €1,100 a month. Milan's north-west corporate leases compress to 3-3.6% on €500,000 stock at €1,800. German and British landlords are effectively choosing between cash flow and the credit quality of a Lombardy corporate tenant. Net spreads after IMU and 21% cedolare secca narrow that gap further, and should be modelled before either figure is trusted.
