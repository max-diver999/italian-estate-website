## What Does a New-Build Masseria Ticket Actually Include?

Rural new-build in Puglia requires a permit chain that foreign buyers rarely see priced into a €380,000 headline. Pool works, access roads, septic systems and boundary walls each carry their own filing, and a masseria marketed as complete is often complete only to the shell-and-services stage.

| Item | Typical position at reservation |
|---|---|
| Pool | Separate SCIA filing; often not yet lodged |
| Access and services | Frequently excluded from the headline price |
| Agricultural land classification | Constrains what may be built on the parcel |
| Handover window | Commonly 12-18 months on rural new-build |

- Ask for the capitolato: what the price includes down to finishes, not what the render shows.
- Pool SCIA remediation on villas marketed as ready commonly runs €25,000 to €60,000.
- Budget IMU levied at 0.86% to 1.06% of the cadastral value annually on a second home.
