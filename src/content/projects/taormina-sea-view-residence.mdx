### What constrains alteration on Ionian sea-view stock?

Taormina's terraces are the feature that carries the price, and they are also where compliance most often fails. Enclosures and extensions added over the years frequently outran the cadastral plan, and any visible alteration on protected slopes needs clearance before works can lawfully begin.

- Terrace conformità is the recurring pre-rogito finding on this stock.
- Establish the clearance position before the offer; approvals here are not quick.
- Short letting needs CIN registration and a condominium that permits tourist use.
