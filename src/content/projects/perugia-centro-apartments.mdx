Two features of this market reward patience. Hospital and university leases renew on a calendar rather than on demand, so a unit bought in November will not let properly until the following September whatever the asking rent — which makes spring the wrong time to buy and the right time to sell. And parking, which almost nothing in the hilltop centro has, is the single amenity that moves a Perugia rent: a bilocale with a deeded space commands 10 to 15 percent more than an identical unit without one, and lets in weeks rather than months. Both effects belong in the model before a Perugia yield is set against a coastal one.
