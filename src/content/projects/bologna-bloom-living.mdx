## What Is Bologna Bloom Living in Navile?

Bloom Living is 145 Class A apartments on Via Giorgio Bassani in Navile, from about €197,000 for a 50-52 sqm monolocale, across two eleven-storey Lombardini22 towers and a four-storey podium, handing over November 2027. Concierge, gym and co-working services are included — Emilia-Romagna new build below Milan's north-west entry.

Prelios promotes the scheme through Fondo Fidia with Abitare Co sales and First Atlantic Real Estate advisory. Buyers purchase Navile regeneration exposure with AV high-speed station proximity, not historic centro palazzo uncertainty inside UNESCO walls.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

The site sits in the Navile canal regeneration zone about 3 km north of the Bologna centro: 10-15 minutes to Bologna Centrale, 15-20 to Piazza Maggiore, 20-25 to Marconi airport. From Centrale the AV reaches Milan in 65-75 minutes and Florence in 35-45.

Hospital and university tenants accept a periphery address where the transport works and the lease package includes a parking deed. That commuter and student pool is what a €197,000 entry here is buying, against [Inspire UpTown Milan](/projects/inspire-uptown-milan/) corporate liquidity at a materially higher price per sqm.

## What Do Service Charges Look Like on New Bologna Stock?

New-build charges in Navile differ in composition from older Bologna buildings rather than simply in amount. Maintenance is lower on recent construction, but service-led items — lifts, shared systems, managed green space — arrive from the first year, so on a €197,000 entry unit the budget starts higher than a comparable pre-1980 block.

| Item | New-build position |
|---|---|
| Ordinary maintenance | Low in early years |
| Service and shared systems | Charged from year 1 |
| Reserve fund | Builds from handover |

Add IMU levied at 0.86% to 1.06% of the cadastral value on a second home. Tenant demand comes from the university and hospital corridors, which suits twelve-month furnished leases.

### Which tenants does Navile actually attract?

Navile sits between the university and the hospital corridors, which gives a €197,000 ticket an unusually stable tenant base for a regeneration district. Demand is academic, clinical and corporate rather than touristic, so the realistic case is a twelve-month furnished lease rather than a short-let calendar.

- Vacancy is materially lower than tourism-led Italian stock, because contracts run a full year.
- Confirm which phase your building sits in: services arrive on the masterplan's schedule, not the reservation date.
- Short letting would need CIN registration and a condominium regolamento that permits it.
