## What Is Bliss UpTown Milan?

Bliss UpTown is on-market stock inside EuroMilano's UpTown masterplan at Cascina Merlata, from €380,000, returning 3.2-3.8% net on a long lease. Metro M1 reaches Duomo in about 15 minutes. Against Inspire UpTown off-plan at €348,500, what the premium buys is a building that already exists.

[Near Milan](/developers/near-milan/) and EuroMilano market Bliss as immediate or near-term stock within the same tower cluster as Inspire (Q2 2028 off-plan) and [Feel UpTown](/projects/feel-uptown-milan/) delivering from 517,000 EUR. Agency-listed units rotate through uptown-milano.it and immobiliare.it, so buyers should verify seller identity and floor exposure before anchoring offer price on portal averages alone.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Cascina Merlata sits in north-west Milan: 15 minutes on the M1 to Duomo, 20 minutes by car to Malpensa, and 10-15 minutes by bike to the MIND innovation district. District retail is still maturing, which is the honest difference against Navigli, where it matured decades ago.

Cascina Merlata grew from Expo 2015 fringe land into a mixed-use corridor anchored by MIND employers including Microsoft Italy and Johnson and Johnson innovation labs. UpTown towers share landscaped public realm, ground-floor retail pods, and M1 station access that preserves commuter value for furnished long-term leases when buyers document walking time from the specific Bliss tower entrance.

## What Should You Know About Investment Case?

The case is 3.2-3.8% net and 3.5-4.5% gross furnished, on €1,600-2,200 monthly rents for two-bedroom stock. What distinguishes Bliss from the rest of the masterplan is timing rather than economics: it is on-market now, against an Inspire UpTown handover in Q2 2028.

Model net yield after IMU, registration tax at 2 percent prima casa or 9 percent second home, and 15 to 20 percent void allowance on furnished leases rather than peak MIND relocation weeks alone. Bliss on-market pricing may sit between Inspire pre-sales and Feel delivering thresholds; buyers who missed Inspire entry should compare lost rent months against any €/sqm premium on immediate availability.

Short-term rental can lift summer gross revenue but requires valid CIN registration, condominium regolamento approval, Alloggiati Web guest filing, and cedolare secca at 21 percent on first property or 26 percent on second. Verify building rules before underwriting STR because northwest Milan new builds often restrict affitti brevi even when long-term furnished leases remain permitted.

Transaction stack on 500,000 EUR second-home purchase runs 8 to 12 percent including registration tax, notary, agency commission on resale paths, and furnishing for corporate-ready two-bedroom stock. Parking often trades separately at 25,000 to 45,000 EUR in EuroMilano schemes and should be modeled in total ticket size before yield calculation.

The Bliss case is the Cascina Merlata case, and it rests on three supports that are external to the building. The M1 metro reaches the Duomo in roughly 15-20 minutes and Malpensa is 20-25 minutes by road. The MIND innovation district next door supplies a corporate and research tenant base whose housing demand is weekday and year-round rather than seasonal. And entry at €4,200-4,800 per sqm sits roughly 35% below central Milan's €6,500-10,000, which is the discount being paid for a district still maturing. Against that: completion in 2028, no resale comparables until handover, and short-let restrictions common in new Milan condominium regolamenti. The realistic model is a twelve-month furnished lease at 3-4% gross with low vacancy across a 7 to 10 year hold, not a short-let yield play.

## How Does Phasing Affect a Bliss Purchase?

Phasing at Cascina Merlata typically decides what a foreign buyer actually receives on handover day. Two units at the same €350,000 ticket can differ by 2 to 3 years of surrounding works, and Class A rents assume a finished district rather than an active site next door.

| Check | Why it moves the number |
|---|---|
| Certified completion date for your phase | The masterplan date can sit 24-36 months later |
| Share of the phase already trading | Amenity pricing is paid at day 1, delivered on the phase schedule |
| Fideiussione cover | Must cover 100% of sums paid before the deed, not the 10-20% deposit |
| Postuma decennale | 10-year structural cover, issued at the rogito |

- Ask which phase the unit belongs to, and for that phase's certified date rather than the scheme headline.
- Confirm what is open around it: a Class A building behind hoardings does not achieve Class A rents.
- Budget annual IMU running 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value from handover, with no primary-residence relief for a second home.
