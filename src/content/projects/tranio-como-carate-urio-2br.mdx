## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Carate Urio sits on the SS583 between Como city and Bellagio: 5-10 minutes on foot to the waterfront, 20-30 by car to Como centro, 15-25 by ferry to Bellagio, 60-75 to Malpensa and 50-70 to Lugano. At €350,000 it is a third of Como lakefront and a fraction of Bellagio.

Swiss and German buyers treat Carate Urio as accessible Lake Como entry below Bellagio trophy tickets while preserving ferry-connected lake lifestyle without Como city urban density. Milan professionals occasionally lease furnished units for weekend lake rotation when broadband and parking meet corporate standards.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Restored 1960s-1990s stock with selective new-build infill, on 65-90 sqm two-bedroom plates oriented toward the Como bay and Bellagio panoramas. Two things move the price disproportionately: whether the building has a lift, which Swiss buyers treat as non-negotiable, and whether the lake view can be obstructed by future tree growth.

Each Tranio listing represents independent promoter or private seller stock. Buyers should verify elevator status, lake-view obstruction risk from future tree growth, and conformità on terrace extensions common in lakeside marketing photography. Top-floor units with elevator access trade toward €550,000 to €800,000 before furnishing.

Parking inclusion materially affects tenant appeal because lakeside village street parking remains competitive during summer tourism weekends. Underground or deeded parking adds €30,000 to €60,000 value on comparable floor plates without garage access.

## How Does Seasonality Shape a Carate Urio Let?

Lake Como letting is strongly seasonal, and foreign buyers routinely model summer rates across 12 months. On a €350,000 two-bedroom the realistic case concentrates most of the year's income into roughly 4 to 5 months, with the remainder carrying full standing costs.

| Line | Reality on this ticket |
|---|---|
| Peak season | Roughly 4-5 months of the year |
| Off-season | Standing costs continue: IMU, spese, utilities |
| CIN registration | Required before advertising tourist use |
| Condominium regolamento | Can prohibit tourist use outright |

- Model annual occupancy rather than peak nightly rate; the two produce very different net yields.
- Confirm tourist use is permitted by both the comune and the building before pricing the summer case.
- Short-let income is taxed at 21% on the first unit and 26% on the second.
