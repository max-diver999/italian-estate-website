## Which Filings Decide a Carovigno Villa?

Three filings typically decide whether a countryside villa near Carovigno can be used the way it is marketed. Foreign buyers at €350,000 should treat the pool SCIA, the agricultural land classification and the conformità of any extension as pre-caparra items, because each of the 3 independently constrains both the permitted use and the eventual resale.

| Filing | Consequence if incomplete |
|---|---|
| Pool SCIA | Remediation commonly €25,000-€60,000 |
| Land classification | Limits further building on the parcel |
| Conformità on extensions | Gap transfers to the buyer at the rogito |

- Clear all 3 before the caparra; none is fixable by renegotiating price afterwards.
- Legal short letting additionally requires CIN registration and the comune's tourist-use approval.
- Carovigno sits inland from the Torre Guaceto reserve, so parcels near the protected coast carry extra siting limits.
- Budget IMU at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value annually on a second home, with no primary-residence relief.
