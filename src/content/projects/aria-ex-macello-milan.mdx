## What Is ARIA Ex Macello Milan?

ARIA is a 150,000 sqm Reinventing Cities district on Milan's former Calvairate slaughterhouse site, led by Redo SGR, targeting carbon-negative operation across 1,200 homes and 600 student beds. For a foreign buyer the important qualification is access: direct freehold inventory is limited, so most participation runs through the fund rather than through a purchase, which makes this a Milan regeneration to track rather than to buy into.

ARIA reactivates the Ex Macello complex decommissioned in the 1990s on Viale Molise in Calvairate. The masterplan allocates roughly 35 percent of new surface to residential uses with the majority social and affordable housing, over 40 percent to mixed functions, and approximately 15 percent of pre-existing structures undergo heritage recovery within Snøhetta-led envelopes.

- **Scale:** 150,000 sqm inside Milan municipality boundaries
- **Residential:** About 1,200 apartments plus 600 PBSA beds
- **Developer:** [Redo SGR](/developers/redo-sgr-italy/) for Fondo Immobiliare Lombardia
- **Completion:** Phased delivery 2026 to 2027 after H1 2026 construction start
- **Compare:** Open-market [Inspire UpTown](/projects/inspire-uptown-milan/) from 348,500 EUR

## What Should You Know About Investment Case?

ARIA is institutional capital rather than a trophy purchase: affordable housing, build-to-rent and student accommodation, backed by Fondo Immobiliare Lombardia with CDP Investimenti SGR and Regione Lombardia as quotisti. Direct inventory stays channel-restricted, so individual foreign buyers generally end up benchmarking against north-west off-plan instead.

| Factor | Benchmark |
| --- | --- |
| Site scale | 150,000 sqm inside Milan municipality |
| Residential volume | About 1,200 units social plus affordable mix |
| PBSA beds | 600 student residence |
| IED campus | 20,000 to 30,000 sqm consolidated |
| Concession cost ref | About 97.8 million USD Sur Atlas |
| Open-market compare | [Inspire UpTown](/projects/inspire-uptown-milan/) freehold |

Build-to-sell phases within social housing frameworks may release subsidized units to eligible households under Italian edilizia residenziale pubblica rules rather than unrestricted foreign cash purchases. Long-term furnished demand from IED students and creative industries may support BTR underwriting on PBSA and mid-market operator-released units.

The ARIA case is a district thesis rather than a unit thesis, and the two need separating before capital is committed. What the scheme offers is institutional delivery at scale — 150,000 sqm, a carbon-negative energy target, an IED campus and 600 student beds anchoring daytime activity in a quarter of southeast Milan that has been industrial for a century. What it does not offer most foreign buyers is straightforward freehold access, because a substantial share of the programme is allocated rather than sold openly. For those who can participate, the return depends on Calvairate repricing toward central Milan levels across a 7 to 10 year hold, against a citywide average near €5,653 per sqm today. That is a credible thesis and a slow one, and it should not be underwritten as a three-year regeneration trade.

## What Does the Ex Macello Regeneration Timeline Mean for Buyers?

Regeneration timing is the variable foreign buyers most often underprice at Calvairate. The Ex Macello scheme reprices surrounding streets over 5 to 10 years, which means the entry comparison today and the exit comparison at year 5 are two different districts trading at two different levels per sqm.

| What to establish | Why it moves the number |
|---|---|
| Certified stage date for your building | Masterplan headline dates can run 24-36 months ahead of a single block |
| What legacy resale nearby trades at | That is the comparison set a valuer uses today, not the rendering |
| Fideiussione cover | Must cover 100% of sums paid before the deed |
| Postuma decennale | 10-year structural cover, issued at the rogito |

- Model the hold from your building's certified date, not from the reservation date.
- Price the exit against the regenerated district and the entry against the current one; the gap is the return.
- Budget IMU at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value from handover, with no primary-residence relief on a second home.
