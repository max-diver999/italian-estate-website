## What Is COIMA Via Carcani Trastevere?

Via Carcani is a COIMA SGR office-to-residential conversion in Trastevere, turning commercial floorplates into Class A apartments eight minutes on foot from Piazza Santa Maria in Trastevere. Restored stock in the district resells at €5,000-8,000 per sqm. The appeal is the address without centro palazzo renovation risk.

The project sits in one of Rome's strongest foreign buyer neighborhoods west of the Tiber, where English-speaking expats, US university students, and EU remote workers cluster around walkable restaurants and centro bridges. COIMA applies pension-mandate ESG standards to MEP upgrades, acoustic separation, and energy envelopes that legacy office cores rarely deliver without full structural rework.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

A Class A conversion out of institutional office volumes, which brings upgraded MEP and — the point most buyers miss — acoustic separation engineered for former commercial use. ESG efficiency targets follow COIMA's fund mandate. Trastevere resale benchmarks of €5,000-8,000 per sqm compare against restored palazzi above €700,000.

Office-to-residential conversions require planning approval, structural engineering for residential standards, and acoustic upgrades versus original commercial use. ## What Is Different About Converting Stock in Trastevere?

Trastevere conversions sit inside a protected quarter, so the constraint is what the authority permits rather than what the design proposes. Non-resident buyers should also read the delivery contract closely: institutional conversions carry penalty clauses, and what those actually pay on a late handover varies widely.

| Question | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| Protected-quarter rules | Constrain external alteration |
| Penalty clauses | Confirm the amount, not just their existence |
| Condominium position on letting | Frequently differs from the comune's |

Conformità on converted stock remains the recurring pre-rogito finding. Annual IMU runs 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value.

One further point separates an office-to-residential conversion from a new build on open ground. The change of use has to be registered at the catasto, moving the unit from category A/10 to a residential category, and the cadastral rendita is reassessed with it. That reassessment sets the IMU bill and the registration tax base for the buyer after you, so ask to see the new rendita rather than the old one before accepting any running-cost projection the sales desk supplies.
