## What Is Amalfi Ravello Villa Stock?

Ravello villa stock is restored garden property on UNESCO headland terraces, from €1,500,000 for 150-280 sqm of living space. Licensed short stays return 2.5-4% gross, which is not why anyone buys here. The asset is resale depth into an American and British market that has been buying this hillside for a century.

Agency portfolios mix turnkey restored terraces with raw casali needing 300,000 to 600,000 EUR MEP and pool remediation behind heritage-compliant facades. Each contrada lane carries distinct geotechnical exposure, parking deeds, and Soprintendenza history that buyers must verify before compromesso on cliff-edge photography.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Ravello sits 350 metres above the sea, which is the whole proposition and the whole constraint. The centro is a 5-15 minute walk, the Amalfi marina a 15-25 minute drive, Naples airport 90-120 minutes. Buyers are trading ferry-side walkability for terrace privacy that nothing at sea level can offer.

Ravello music festival weeks and July-August Gulf tourism support event-heavy short-stay calendars when villas include staffed kitchens and documented parking for guest transfers on SS163 cliff roads. Shoulder seasons can host wedding and corporate retreat packages on terraces with verified conformità.

| Destination | Approximate time |
| --- | --- |
| Ravello centro / Villa Rufolo | 5 to 15 min walk |
| Amalfi town marina | 15 to 25 min drive |
| Positano cliff road | 45 to 60 min |
| Sorrento ferries | 60 to 75 min |
| Naples Capodichino | 90 to 120 min |

Headland villas command 15 to 25 percent per sqm premiums over compact Sorrento centro units on comparable 150 sqm stock in Amalfi comparables. SS163 summer congestion adds 45 to 60 minutes to Naples airport transfers during peak August weeks when daily vehicle counts exceed 8,000 on cliff segments. Owners marketing 2,000 to 5,000 EUR nightly short-stay should document helicopter and private transfer partners for ultra-HNWI guest expectations on cliff-road logistics.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

The stock tiers cleanly. Restored villas of 150-250 sqm with terrace gardens and compliant infinity pools run €1,500,000-3,000,000; trophy terraces €3,000,000-8,000,000 and above; raw casali need €250,000-600,000 of remediation. Anything visible from a public viewpoint carries 6-12 months of Soprintendenza review before work starts.

Inventory spans restored music-festival-week villas with mature planting to renovation shells marketed with drone photography before filed SCIA exists. Buyers should treat terrace infinity pool renders as conditional until geometra confirms vincolo paesaggistico clearance and insurance acceptance on frana-exposed contrada.

## What Should You Know About Investment Case?

Licensed short stays on €1,800,000-3,500,000 tickets return 2.5-4% gross, at €2,000-5,000 a night in peak summer. July and August alone carry 45-55% of annual gross. The exit is the harder number: 18-36 months of marketing is normal, so the hold has to be long by design rather than by accident.

| Factor | Benchmark |
| --- | --- |
| STR gross (trophy villa) | 2.5 to 4 percent |
| Summer nightly peak | 2,000 to 5,000 EUR |
| Jul-Aug revenue share | 45 to 55 percent of annual gross |
| Rentable nights model | 120 to 140 nights per year |
| Management (staffed STR) | 25 to 35 percent of gross |
| Resale liquidity | 18 to 36 months typical |
| Compare gateway | [Sorrento from 350k EUR](/projects/sorrento-coastal-apartment/) |

Model 120 to 140 rentable nights annually rather than calendar-year occupancy claims in agency pro formas. Capital preservation dominates yield thesis: correctly priced garden villas with geotechnical clearance may wait 18 to 36 months for qualified HNWI buyer versus months on lower-ticket Sorrento apartments with broader Anglo-American resale audience.

Transaction stack on 2,000,000 EUR second-home purchase runs 10 to 15 percent including registration tax, notary, geotechnical survey, and heritage consultancy fees. Ultra-luxury short-stay launch adds 40,000 to 80,000 EUR for designer furniture and concierge onboarding before first guest season.

Non-resident buyers rarely secure Italian mortgage leverage on 1.5M EUR plus cliff villas because banks underwrite geotechnical risk slowly when rental income is ultra-seasonal short-stay. Cash or cross-border mortgage buyers dominate Ravello transactions, which speeds rogito to 90 to 120 days when geotechnical and conformità packets are complete pre-compromesso.

This stock suits three buyers. Trophy collectors who accept 2.5-4% gross and sub-20% winter occupancy; American and British buyers who specifically want an Amalfi garden address; and portfolio buyers pairing a headland villa with something liquid — a Sorrento gateway apartment — to balance the concentration.

- **Trophy HNWI:** Garden terraces, Villa Rufolo views, capital preservation thesis
- **Event STR operators:** Music festival and wedding calendars on staffed villas
- **Gateway plus trophy:** Sorrento cash-flow layer with Ravello step-up timing
- **Lifestyle primary:** Accepting UNESCO permit timelines on terrace projects

| STR pro forma | 120 to 140 nights modeled, not calendar occupancy |

## What Governs Works on a Ravello Villa?

Landscape protection is the binding constraint on the Amalfi Coast, and at a €1,500,000 ticket it applies to almost everything a foreign buyer would want to change. Terraces, pools, openings and parking on protected slopes require clearance before any credible works programme or letting plan can be priced.

| Element | Constraint |
|---|---|
| Terraces and pools | Landscape vincolo; clearance required |
| External openings | Restricted on protected historic stock |
| Access and parking | Frequently non-assignable on cliff sites |
| Tourist use | Comune rules plus CIN registration |

- Establish the Soprintendenza position before the offer; approvals on this coast routinely add 12 to 18 months.
- Confirm parking rights in the deed: non-assignable arrangements can cost €12,000 to €28,000 a year to replace.
- Budget IMU of 0.86% to 1.06%, charged on cadastral value annually on a second home.
