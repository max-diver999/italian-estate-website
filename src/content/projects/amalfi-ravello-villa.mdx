## What comes up for sale in Ravello

Ravello villa stock is restored garden property on UNESCO headland terraces, from €1,500,000 for 150-280 sqm of living space. Licensed short stays return 2.5-4% gross, which is not why anyone buys here. The asset is resale depth into an American and British market that has been buying this hillside for a century.

Agency portfolios mix turnkey restored terraces with raw casali needing 300,000 to 600,000 EUR MEP and pool remediation behind heritage-compliant facades. Each contrada lane carries distinct geotechnical exposure, parking deeds, and Soprintendenza history that buyers must verify before compromesso on cliff-edge photography.

## The shape of the market

Resale stock starts at €1,500,000 for three to five bedrooms across 150-280 sqm. Exterior works fall under UNESCO vincolo. Letting requires both a national CIN and a Campania regional CIR. And the number most buyers underestimate: a correctly priced garden villa takes 18-36 months to sell.

Entry is EUR 1,500,000 for something between 150 and 280 square metres, usually three to five bedrooms, and usually resale that wants work rather than a finished house. Letting requires both the national CIN and Campania's regional CIR. Everything visible from outside sits under the Costiera Amalfitana landscape designation. A buyer who finds the entry ticket too high normally ends up looking at [Sorrento apartments](/projects/sorrento-coastal-apartment/) instead, which is a different asset at a fifth of the price.

## Where Ravello sits, and what the road costs you

Ravello sits 350 metres above the sea, which is the whole proposition and the whole constraint. The centro is a 5-15 minute walk, the Amalfi marina a 15-25 minute drive, Naples airport 90-120 minutes. On a 150 sqm villa a buyer trades ferry-side walkability for terrace privacy that nothing at sea level can offer.

Ravello music festival weeks and July-August Gulf tourism support event-heavy short-stay calendars when villas include staffed kitchens and documented parking for guest transfers on SS163 cliff roads. Shoulder seasons can host wedding and corporate retreat packages on terraces with verified conformità.

Most addresses are five to fifteen minutes on foot from the centro and Villa Rufolo. The Amalfi marina is a fifteen to twenty-five minute drive down the hill, Positano forty-five minutes to an hour along the cliff road, the Sorrento ferries an hour to an hour and a quarter, and Naples airport ninety minutes to two hours on a good day.

Per square metre, a headland villa here carries 15% to 25% over a compact Sorrento centro unit of comparable size, which is what the address costs before anything else is counted. Through the August peak the SS163 adds the best part of an hour to any transfer to Naples Capodichino, and the road is the only one there is. Owners marketing 2,000 to 5,000 EUR nightly short-stay should document helicopter and private transfer partners for ultra-HNWI guest expectations on cliff-road logistics.

## The three tiers of stock

The stock tiers cleanly. Restored villas of 150-250 sqm with terrace gardens and compliant infinity pools run €1,500,000-3,000,000; trophy terraces €3,000,000-8,000,000 and above; raw casali need €250,000-600,000 of remediation. Anything visible from a public viewpoint carries 6-12 months of Soprintendenza review before work starts.

Inventory spans restored music-festival-week villas with mature planting to renovation shells marketed with drone photography before filed SCIA exists. Treat a render of a terrace infinity pool as a proposal rather than a feature until a geometra has confirmed clearance under the vincolo paesaggistico.

## Is there anything here below the villa tier

This is the question most searches for houses in Ravello are really asking, and the honest answer starts with the size of the place.

Ravello is a comune of roughly 2,500 people occupying a headland. The centro is small enough to cross in ten minutes, and the rest of the comune is a handful of frazioni scattered down the hillside: Torello, Sambuco, Sant'Egidio, Lacco, and Marmorata at sea level below. There is no suburb, no new development, and no room to build one, because the whole landscape sits under the Costiera Amalfitana designation.

That produces a market with two consequences. Supply in any given month is measured in a handful of properties rather than in pages of listings, and what reaches international marketing is almost entirely the villa tier, because that is what supports the cost of marketing it abroad.

Below that tier the stock exists but circulates locally: apartments in and just outside the centro, terraced houses in the frazioni, and property that comes to the market through inheritance rather than through a decision to sell. It is reachable, but it is found by being on the ground and in front of local agents rather than by refreshing a portal from abroad.

Two practical points follow for anyone whose budget sits below the trophy level. The first is that the frazioni are a real alternative rather than a compromise: Torello and Sambuco carry the same views and the same designation, with a walk or a drive between them and the centro. The second is that the constraints do not scale down with the price. A small house on the hillside carries the same landscape designation, the same geotechnical questions and the same permitting timelines as a villa ten times its value, and the survey costs very nearly the same.

Anyone who wants the coast at a lower entry and a deeper market is genuinely better served a headland away, which is what the [Amalfi Coast guide](/guides/amalfi-coast-property-investment-guide/) sets out and what the Sorrento comparison in this page keeps returning to.

## What it returns, and why that is not the point

A licensed short let on a ticket between €1.8M and €3.5M returns 2.5% to 4% gross, built from nightly rates of €2,000 to €5,000 across a short peak summer. July and August alone carry 45-55% of annual gross. The exit is the harder number: 18-36 months of marketing is normal, so the hold has to be long by design rather than by accident.

The numbers to underwrite on: 2.5% to 4% gross on a trophy villa, nightly rates of EUR 2,000 to EUR 5,000 at the summer peak, July and August carrying 45% to 55% of the annual take, 120 to 140 lettable nights across the year rather than a full calendar, management at 25% to 35% of gross on a staffed property, and eighteen months to three years to sell when the time comes. The liquid comparison, again, is [Sorrento from EUR 350,000](/projects/sorrento-coastal-apartment/).

Underwrite on 120 to 140 lettable nights a year. An agency pro forma quoting occupancy against a full calendar is describing a coastline that closes for half of it. This is a capital preservation asset rather than a yield one: a correctly priced garden villa with geotechnical clearance can wait eighteen months to three years for the one buyer who wants it, where a Sorrento apartment at a fifth of the ticket sells to a far wider Anglo-American audience in months.

At this level the costs on top of the price run between a tenth and a seventh: registration tax, the notaio, a geotechnical survey the terrain makes non-negotiable, and heritage consultancy. Before the first guest arrives, budget another 40,000 to 80,000 EUR for furnishing to the standard this market expects and for onboarding a concierge operation.

Non-resident buyers rarely secure Italian mortgage leverage on 1.5M EUR plus cliff villas because banks underwrite geotechnical risk slowly when rental income is ultra-seasonal short-stay. Almost nobody buys here on an Italian mortgage. Purchases settle in cash or through cross-border lending, and where the geotechnical and conformità packets are assembled before the compromesso a rogito lands inside three to four months.

The Ravello case is a lifestyle case with income attached, and stating it that way avoids the disappointment that follows the opposite framing. Weekly summer rates on a terraced villa with a view are genuinely high, but the season concentrates into roughly June to September with shoulder weeks either side, and management on a property of this type runs 20-25% of gross before cleaning. Standing costs do not follow the season: IMU at 0.86-1.06% of cadastral value, garden and pool maintenance, and insurance on a house empty for much of the year. Against that sits an address with almost no substitutable supply and a resale pool that is international rather than local. Underwrite it as capital preservation with 2-4% gross of offsetting income, and it performs; underwrite it on August rates annualised, and it will not.

## Who buys here

This stock suits three buyers. Trophy collectors who accept 2.5-4% gross and sub-20% winter occupancy on €1,800,000-3,500,000 tickets; American and British buyers who specifically want an Amalfi garden address and will wait 18-36 months to resell it; and portfolio buyers pairing a headland villa with something liquid, such as a Sorrento gateway apartment from €350,000.

- **Trophy HNWI:** Garden terraces, Villa Rufolo views, capital preservation thesis
- **Event STR operators:** Music festival and wedding calendars on staffed villas
- **Gateway plus trophy:** Sorrento cash-flow layer with Ravello step-up timing
- **Lifestyle primary:** Accepting UNESCO permit timelines on terrace projects

## What Should Foreign Buyers Verify Before Reserving?

Six items before compromesso on a €1,500,000-8,000,000 ticket. The relazione geologica and PAI landslide classification; a geometra audit of terrace and pool conformità; confirmation of the Soprintendenza path for planned works; the parking deed; a short-stay model built on 120-140 nights rather than a full year; and independent avvocato review.

| What goes wrong here | What settles it |
| --- | --- |
| Geotechnical gap | Relazione geologica and PAI frana zones before deposit |
| Terrace conformità | Pool and retaining walls match cadastral and SCIA records |
| UNESCO exposure | Soprintendenza timeline on visible exterior works |
| Parking deed | Legal bays on narrow contrada lanes |
| STR pro forma | 120 to 140 nights modeled, not calendar occupancy |
| Access rights | SS163 and private lane maintenance agreements |

Work in this order. Secure the codice fiscale and settle the notary-led wire path before the compromesso. Put a geometra on the terraces, the pool and the drainage, which on a cliff plot is where the money and the refusals both sit, and read [what a heritage constraint actually restricts](/guides/heritage-restricted-property-italy/) before drawing any exterior plan.

Then the letting and the money. Confirm the CIN and the Campania CIR path through [Italy holiday let licensing](/guides/italy-holiday-let-licensing/) rather than assuming a licence transfers, and model the net after IMU and short-stay tax with a commercialista using the [Italy rental yield guide](/guides/italy-rental-yield-guide/). Have encumbrances read by [your own avvocato](/guides/due-diligence-italy-property/), compare headland tickets against the pricing bands in the [Sorrento area guide](/areas/sorrento/), and take the full order of operations from our [guide for foreign buyers](/guides/buy-property-italy-foreigner/), adding the landscape vincolo to the pre-caparra file.

Argue the price from three sales that have completed in the same contrada. Spring listings on this coast open well above the OMI band for the comune, so an asking price is where the conversation starts rather than where it lands.

## What Governs Works on a Ravello Villa?

Landscape protection is the binding constraint on the Amalfi Coast, and at a €1,500,000 ticket it applies to almost everything a foreign buyer would want to change. Terraces, pools, openings and parking on protected slopes require clearance before any credible works programme or letting plan can be priced.

| What you may change | What limits it |
|---|---|
| Terraces and pools | Landscape vincolo; clearance required |
| External openings | Restricted on protected historic stock |
| Access and parking | Frequently non-assignable on cliff sites |
| Tourist use | Comune rules plus CIN registration |

- Establish the Soprintendenza position before the offer; approvals on this coast routinely add 12 to 18 months.
- Confirm parking rights in the deed: non-assignable arrangements can cost €12,000 to €28,000 a year to replace.
- IMU on a second home is charged annually at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value, with none of the primary-residence relief.

Ravello sits inside the Amalfi Coast UNESCO landscape, and that designation governs works more tightly than the comune alone would. Anything visible from the coastal viewpoints (roof line, render colour, terrace structures, pergolas and pool surrounds) needs landscape authorisation before a contractor mobilises, and the Soprintendenza timeline runs months rather than weeks. The terracing that makes these gardens saleable is itself the constraint: retaining walls at 350 metres above the sea are structural elements, not garden features, and their repair is an engineering job. On a €1,000,000-plus villa the practical sequence is to price the works from a geometra survey and a landscape opinion before the offer, not from a builder's estimate afterwards, because the difference between what is buildable and what is drawn is where Amalfi budgets fail.
