## What Happens to the Village After the Games?

Post-Games conversion, not the event, is what a foreign buyer is really purchasing at Porta Romana. The Village was built for the 2026 Games and converts afterwards, so the handover date that matters sits after conversion and the resale pool depends on which of the two end uses a block takes.

| Question | Why it decides the ticket |
|---|---|
| Open-market or student block? | The 2 uses resell to different buyer pools |
| Post-conversion handover date | The Games date is not the delivery date |
| Railway yard masterplan phasing | Services continue arriving after 2026 |

- Student-adjacent blocks let reliably but resell to a narrower pool; settle that trade-off before the caparra.
- Porta Romana pricing already reflects the completed masterplan, so the upside is delivery, not discovery.
- Budget IMU at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value from handover, with no relief on a second home.

Post-Games conversion is the whole investment relevance of this site, and the mechanics matter more than the Olympic branding. The village completed construction ahead of schedule in 2025 and housed athletes through the Games window; conversion into student housing targets an August 2026 opening for the 2026/27 academic year, offering about 1,700 beds across roughly 1,200 rooms and marketed as Italy's largest affiliated student residence. Bocconi holds substantial allocations, and a share of the beds is subsidised under Ministry compliance. For a foreign buyer the consequence is straightforward: none of this is condominium stock for sale. The investable exposure is adjacent Porta Romana property, whose case rests on 1,700 students arriving in a quarter that did not previously have them — a genuine demand catalyst, priced into spillover stock at 2.5-3.5% gross.
