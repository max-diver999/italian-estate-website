## What Is Tranio Florence Historic Center Stock?

This is restored apartments in the UNESCO palazzi north of the Arno, within walking distance of the Duomo and the museum corridors, from about €500,000 for 45-80 sqm. New short-let licences in the centro are banned, so the only route to tourist income is a transferable CIN attached to specific resale stock.

Each listing represents independent seller inventory rather than a single developer pipeline. Buyers purchase into a regulated heritage market where Soprintendenza oversight, terrace legality, and elevator status materially affect pricing and resale liquidity on tickets US and UK enquiry dominates each academic season.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

The relevant quarters are Duomo, San Lorenzo, Santa Croce and Piazza della Repubblica, all 5-15 minutes on foot from the museum corridors. Santa Maria Novella station is 10-20 minutes away, Peretola airport 25-35, Chianti 40-60. Pedestrian credentials are the entire asset here, and suburban Florence has none of them.

- **Museum walkability:** Duomo and Piazza del Signoria within 5-15 minutes on foot
- **Rail access:** Santa Maria Novella high-speed connections to Rome and Milan
- **Airport:** Peretola 25-35 minutes; Pisa alternative for budget carriers
- **Oltrarno contrast:** South bank from €400,000 with somewhat easier STR paths

Centro positioning delivers immediate museum-city branding for resale marketing versus suburban Florence stock lacking pedestrian UNESCO credentials foreign buyers search on international portals each spring listing season.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Layouts run 45-80 sqm, one to two bedrooms, inside centuries-old palazzi with timber beams and often only partial electrical upgrades. Terrace status varies from legally registered rooftop access to enclosures that need geometra remediation, and the difference is not visible in photographs. Lift-served floors command 10-20% over third-floor walk-ups — a premium driven by American and British buyers who will not take the stairs whatever the interior finish.

- **Compact 1-bed:** 45-55 sqm from €500,000, core couple and academic tenant format
- **Restored 2-bed:** 60-80 sqm €600,000-900,000 with kitchen-bathroom modernization
- **Premium terrace:** Legal terrazze €900,000-1,500,000 with Duomo glimpses when permitted
- **Walk-up discount:** Third floor without lift trades 10-20% below comparable lifted units

Heritage fabric behind UNESCO facades often combines post-2000 MEP upgrades that fail STR insurance requirements until geometra certifies load and fire egress paths before professional operators accept bookings.

Long furnished leases to diplomats and academics return 2-3.5% gross; a transferable short-let permit under professional management reaches 3-5%. On €600,000-900,000 tickets this is capital preservation with an income offset, supported by UNESCO status that guarantees no competing new supply will ever be built.

Florence centro suits two buyers and the licence position separates them entirely. The first wants the strongest UNESCO address in Italy for personal use, accepts a furnished long lease at 3.5-4.5% gross, and values fixed supply inside a perimeter that cannot expand. The second is specifically buying an existing short-let permit that transfers with the property, which the licence freeze has made a scarce asset worth a real premium over an identical neighbour without one. Both face the same cost base: Soprintendenza authorisation on any external work, timber and electrical systems in centuries-old palazzi, frequent absence of a lift discounting upper floors 10-20%, and IMU at 0.86-1.06%. On €500,000-1.5M stock, confirm the licence before negotiating rather than after.

## What Does Protected Fabric Cost to Own in Florence?

Protection raises both halves of the running cost in the historic centre. Ordinary maintenance is more expensive on historic fabric, and extraordinary programmes — façades, roofs, structural works — arrive on a schedule the authority influences rather than the owners alone.

- Assume tourist letting is restricted in the UNESCO centro until confirmed otherwise in writing.
- Conformità between the built layout and the cadastral plan is the check that most often fails.
- Budget €8,000 to €15,000 of due diligence at this level of purchase.

Condominium charges typically run €1,500 to €3,000 a year, on top of IMU, set between 0.86% and 1.06% of cadastral value by the comune.
