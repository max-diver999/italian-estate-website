## What Is Valle d'Itria Trulli Restoration Stock?

Trulli restoration stock is heritage cone complexes on contrada land: €120,000-350,000 to acquire and from €200,000 to restore, typically 60-80 sqm across two or three cones. Licensed short lets return 5-7% gross. Dutch and British buyers dominate, and Apulia Deluxe runs most of the coordinated heritage programmes.

Apulia Deluxe employs local maestri trullari for chiancarelle roofs and lime mortar work rather than generic contractors, reducing long-term maintenance failures common in poorly restored tourist rentals. Each site carries distinct Soprintendenza approval scope, agricultural land category, and pool feasibility that buyers must verify before compromesso on collapsed cone stock.

## Who Is This For?

Three buyers. Heritage collectors who accept 12-24 months of restoration before any income; British and Dutch short-let investors keeping total capital under €400,000; and lifestyle renovators who want hands-on involvement while a specialist manages the Soprintendenza side of it.

- **Heritage collector:** Authentic UNESCO dry-stone cones with chiancarelle roofs
- **Value STR investor:** Total capital under 400,000 EUR targeting 5 to 7 percent gross
- **Lifestyle renovator:** 18-month horizon with developer-coordinated heritage permits
- **Puglia portfolio entry:** Below 800,000 EUR masseria and trulli-villa flagships

Trulli restoration suits a buyer with restoration appetite and disappoints one without it, so the profiles matter more here than on new-build stock. It fits an owner who wants architecture that exists nowhere else in Europe, entering at €120,000-350,000 and budgeting €800-1,500 per sqm of works on top — a figure that frequently exceeds the purchase price itself. It fits a short-let operator taking a restored cone for 5-7% gross across a season concentrated between May and October, with German and British guests at 65-75% peak occupancy. And it fits a buyer choosing Locorotondo or Cisternino at 20-30% below Ostuni deliberately rather than by budget. It does not fit anyone needing income soon: the structural engineer, the cadastral regularisation and the Soprintendenza clearance all sit ahead of the first booking.

## How Should Restoration Capex Be Sized Here?

Restoration cost, not purchase price, is what decides a Valle d'Itria ticket. Foreign buyers at around €200,000 are typically buying raw or partially restored stone stock where works commonly run €800 to €1,500 per sqm, which usually exceeds whatever the purchase price appeared to save.

| Line | Typical band |
|---|---|
| Restoration | €800-€1,500 per sqm |
| Cadastral regularisation | €3,000-€8,000 |
| Static engineer review | €2,000-€5,000 |
| Licensing to let | CIN registration plus comune approval |

- Size the ticket from the works budget first, then check what is left for the purchase.
- An incomplete condono or conformità path constrains the works programme and the exit together.
- Budget IMU at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value annually on a second home.
