## What Is Rapallo Sea View Apartment Stock?

Rapallo sea-view stock is restored Levante Riviera bilocale from €450,000 with partial or full Gulf terraces, on 65-95 sqm. Licensed short stays return 3-4.5% gross from April to October. British and Swiss buyers dominate, drawn by Portofino adjacency at a fraction of Santa Margherita's harbour pricing.

Agency portfolios mix turnkey promenade stock with castle hillside units needing drainage remediation and terrace conformità review on 1980s coastal towers. Each listing carries distinct parking deed status, elevator compliance, and CIN transfer history that buyers must verify before compromesso on sea-view photography shot from selective angles.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Rapallo sits between Genoa's urban scale and Santa Margherita's marina prestige, with its own castle promontory doing the branding. Genoa is 30-40 minutes by rail, Portofino 15-20 minutes east. Entry runs 10-20% below Santa Margherita per sqm, which is the entire reason to look here.

Portofino regatta weekends and Santa Margherita yacht events spill licensed short-stay demand onto Rapallo sea terraces during April-October when CIN and building regolamento permit tourist sublets. Genoa Brignole commuters accept Rapallo coast weekends with twelve-month furnished lease fallback when abroad on port-sector contracts.

Rapallo's inventory is broad and its quality varies more than the price does, which makes building-level checks decisive. Most sea-view stock sits in 1960s to 1980s blocks where the lift, the facade and the terrace waterproofing approach replacement together, and the assembly votes those as extraordinary assessments rather than spreading them across years. Salt air shortens every one of those cycles. Three documents settle the position: three years of administrator accounts, the minutes showing approved but unspent programmes, and a geometra's view on the terrace membrane. Add the parking arrangement, which on the Tigullio is scarce enough to be a real revenue variable, and the CIN status if letting is planned.
