The metro corridor and Politecnico proximity supply the tenants. Against Milan off-plan at €348,500, Turin buys more floor area and better cash flow with less resale depth.

Crocetta combines elegant condominiums and river proximity without Aurora industrial character. ## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Stock is 1930s-1960s condominium, mostly restored: monolocale at €220,000-300,000, bilocale €280,000-400,000, trilocale with Po-view terraces €400,000-600,000. On anything pre-1970 the shared boiler system usually needs a conformità filing, and that cost should sit in the offer rather than the surprise column.

Inventory spans fully restored units with APE upgrades to partial renovation shells requiring 15,000 to 35,000 EUR MEP remediation on older towers. Buyers should treat marketed turnkey status as conditional until three-year administrator spese history and lift certificate expiry attach to notary bundles before compromesso deposits on pre-1970 stock with pending modernization votes.

## What Do 1960s Crocetta Buildings Cost to Maintain?

Building age is what drives the carry in Crocetta, considerably more than the address does. Blocks from the 1960s typically reach the point where lifts, façades and heating systems all need replacing together, and foreign buyers meet those as extraordinary assessments voted by the assembly rather than as predictable annual charges.

| Programme | Typical exposure |
|---|---|
| Lift modernisation | €8,000-€15,000 |
| Façade works | Voted by the assembly, transfers to the buyer |
| Heating conversion | Increasingly common on this stock |

Request three years of spese history and the assemblea minutes before the caparra. IMU runs 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value.
