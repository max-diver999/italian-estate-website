## What Is Palermo Centro Renovation Apartment Stock?

Palermo centro stock is apartments in the historic centre and the Kalsa baroque quarters, from €180,000 for 50-90 sqm near Quattro Canti, either renovated or awaiting it. Restored units at €200,000-350,000 return 4-6% gross on short lets. Raw palazzo stock needs €60,000-150,000 of works.

Palermo centro storico delivers Baroque architecture, street-food tourism, and apartment tickets that remain among Italy's most accessible major-city entries for yield-focused foreign buyers. Tranio Sicily portfolios list palazzo floors and lane apartments with fresco fragments, tall ceilings, and renovation needs ranging from cosmetic refresh to full MEP replacement.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

The relevant streets are the Quattro Canti and Via Maqueda lanes, the Kalsa waterfront quarters and the Albergheria market corridors, all 5-15 minutes on foot from the cathedral. Palermo airport is 35-45 minutes away and Mondello beach 20-30. Everything inside that walking radius trades at €180,000-350,000 and lets on food tourism; anything outside it competes on price alone.

Centro positioning supports walkable food-tourism STR without car dependency unlike hillside Sicily resort stock, though parking remains valuable for guests exploring western Sicily day trips. Kalsa and waterfront quarters command premium STR photography versus Albergheria lanes where street-level perception still affects guest review scores.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Palazzo floor plates with fresco fragments and tall ceilings, 50-90 sqm at entry level, one or two bedrooms after renovation. Terrace and attic subdivisions frequently need geometra conformità. Third-floor walk-ups trade 15-25% below buildings with a lift, and baroque façade protection limits exterior change entirely.

Palazzo interiors often combine UNESCO-listed facades with layouts requiring modernization for STR insurance and guest comfort standards. Illegal terrace enclosures and attic subdivisions appear frequently in agency listings. Buyers should treat absent conformità documentation as price negotiation leverage rather than post-rogito surprise remediation.

Licensed STR requires CIN registration and Palermo commune compliance with display rules on portals. Some palazzi restrict sub-30-day lets in condominium minutes despite tourism pressure on ground-floor commercial units.

## What Should Foreign Buyers Verify Before Reserving?

Five checks on renovation stock. A full geometra conformità before compromesso, not after; the legality of terrace, attic and room subdivisions; the CIN and Palermo comune registration path; the condition of the palazzo stairwell, roof and shared structure; and €3,000-6,000 of soundproofing where a ground-floor restaurant sits below.

## Why Are Palermo Centro Prices So Low?

Low entry prices in the Palermo centro usually reflect outstanding works rather than a mispriced market. At €800-1,600 per sqm, historic buildings carry extraordinary programmes that the assembly votes and the buyer inherits, so the meaningful number is the purchase price plus whatever has already been approved.

- Request three years of spese history and the assemblea minutes before the caparra.
- A straordinaria vote already passed transfers with the flat at the rogito.
- Survey seismic classification and conformità together rather than sequentially.

IMU on a second home runs 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value, with condominium charges typically €800 to €1,600.
