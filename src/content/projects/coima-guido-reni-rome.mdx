## What Is the COIMA Guido Reni Rome Project?

Guido Reni is COIMA's redevelopment of former military barracks on a five-hectare Flaminio site opposite MAXXI, budgeted at roughly €400 million. The programme mixes residential, student housing, a hotel, retail and a Science Museum. Demolition starts October 2026 against a 2029 completion target.

CDP Real Asset SGR selected COIMA SGR as industrial partner after a 2025 tender with Eagle Hills co-development through the COIMA ESG City Impact Fund backed by Cassa Forense, ENPAM, Intesa Sanpaolo, and Compagnia di San Paolo. Phase one residential release may span 200 to 400 units across 80 to 140 sqm bands with 15,000 sqm underground parking serving roughly 1.2 to 1.5 slots per unit on masterplan reporting. Each sales phase will carry distinct escrow structure, parking allocation, and condominium draft rules that buyers must verify before reservation on institutional regeneration tickets.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Flaminio is Rome's cultural corridor rather than its historic centre — MAXXI is opposite, the Auditorium Parco della Musica is walkable, and the Vatican and centro are 15-25 minutes by transport. Fiumicino is 45-60 minutes. What a buyer avoids here is every UNESCO constraint that applies inside the Aurelian walls.

A new public carriageway from Via Guido Reni to Via del Vignola dissolves the former barracks perimeter and stitches the block into Flaminio pedestrian networks. Green space extends public realm around MAXXI where Grande MAXXI expansion works continue, supporting event-heavy footfall unlike pure tourist centro stock with seasonal void risk.

## What Governs Delivery on an Institutional Rome Scheme?

Institutional developers publish stage schedules, and on a Flaminio ticket those are what a foreign buyer is really underwriting. The relevant date is the certified completion of your own building rather than the masterplan headline, and the guarantee behind it matters more than the brochure.

- The fideiussione must cover every sum paid before the deed, not the reservation alone.
- The ten-year postuma decennale structural policy is handed over at the rogito.
- Flaminio demand is institutional and long-let, so model a twelve-month furnished lease.

From handover, IMU applies at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value on a second home.
