## What Can a Rome Centro Condominium Forbid?

The most consequential document on a Rome centro purchase is not the deed but the condominium regolamento. It can prohibit tourist use outright, and buyers in Municipio I regularly discover the prohibition after paying a caparra on a €450,000-1.4M ticket underwritten at 3-5% gross short-let income.

- Read the assemblea minutes before the caparra: a works vote already passed comes with the flat.
- Inherited abusi edilizi surface late and become the buyer's problem after the rogito.
- Budget €8,000 to €15,000 of due diligence on a purchase around €700,000.

Annual carry is IMU, set between 0.86% and 1.06% of cadastral value by the comune plus spese typically €1,500 to €3,000.
