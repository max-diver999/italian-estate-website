Barolo village is 15-25 minutes from Alba's restaurants and 50-70 minutes from Turin's employment basin, which makes weekend use practical and commuting impractical. Non-resident buyers rarely secure Italian mortgage leverage on UNESCO hill cascini because banks underwrite agricultural land and seasonal income slowly. Cash or cross-border mortgage buyers dominate Langhe transactions, which speeds rogito to 90 to 120 days when land registry and SCIA packets are complete pre-compromesso.

A Langhe vineyard is an operating farm with a house attached, and the investment case has to be built on that order rather than the reverse. Premium Nebbiolo rows inside the Barolo and Barbaresco boundaries trade at €250,000-600,000 per hectare, which prices the appellation rather than the soil, and the vines require work every season whether or not the owner is present. Income comes from three streams that behave differently: bottled wine at 10% VAT plus excise, tastings at €15-35 per seated visitor, and agriturismo rooms where the licensing supports them — the combination lifting gross returns 150-250 basis points over bottle sales alone. Against that sit vintage risk, hail and frost mitigation now standard in Piedmont, and financing at 30-50% loan-to-value for non-residents. Underwrite it as a business with real estate underneath.

## What Is Different About Buying a Vineyard Parcel?

Land classification, not nationality, is what constrains foreign buyers in the Langhe. A €650,000 cascina transfers its residential structure normally, while the attached agricultural parcels follow the land cadastre and require commercialista and avvocato review before an offer is made.

| Element | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Residential structure | Transfers on the ordinary route |
| Agricultural parcels | Land cadastre rules; review before offer |
| Vineyard valuation | Depends on documented harvest history |
| UNESCO landscape | Constrains visible works on hillside plots |

- A perizia without 3 documented harvest seasons is the usual reason a vineyard valuation fails.
- Geotechnical and UNESCO compliance on terrace and pool projects commonly runs €40,000 to €90,000.
- Budget IMU at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value annually on the residential element.

A vineyard parcel is an agricultural asset wrapped in real estate, and three things separate it from buying a house with a view. First, land law: a raw fondo rustico transfer triggers pre-emption notification to neighbouring farmers under Law 161/2014, and non-EU buyers without coltivatore diretto status face additional constraints, which is why most foreign purchases run through the built estate rather than the bare land. Second, appellation: planted rows inside a Barolo or Barbaresco boundary carry yield limits per hectare and consorzio compliance that travel with the title, and premium Nebbiolo rows trade at €250,000-600,000 per hectare on that basis. Third, operation: vines require work every year whether or not the owner is present. Establish all three with a geometra and an agricultural avvocato before an offer rather than after.
