## What Should Foreign Buyers Verify Before Reserving?

Five checks, and two are specific to this coast. Terrace and pool conformità on the visura; mooring rights stated in writing wherever boat access is marketed; an engineer's report on salt-exposed concrete; the coastal planning overlay on cliff parcels; and the regolamento inside condominium subdivisions. All of it before compromesso on €700,000+ tickets.

- Terrace and pool conformità on visura and geometric survey
- Mooring rights confirmed in writing if marketed with boat access
- Engineer inspection on sea-exposed concrete and metal fixtures
- Flood and coastal planning overlays on cliff stock
- Regolamento review if villa sits inside condominium subdivisions

## What Should You Know About Financing?

Italian banks lend non-residents 50-60% LTV against a perizia, and on €700,000-1.5M villa tickets the process runs three to four months from offer to rogito. The risk worth naming: an appraisal below the agreed price cuts the loan without creating any automatic right to renegotiate. Budget 10-12% closing alongside mortgage fees.

Stress-test any short-let pro forma at 50% of peak occupancy.

Regatta and yacht-event weekends can lift nightly rates 30-50% above base July pricing when CIN is active and calendars are marketed early to marina concierge networks. Operators who only list on generic STR platforms miss yacht-week bookings that fill through Porto Ercole berth contacts.

Shoulder weeks in May and October reward operators who package wine routes in Montalcino and Scansano day trips, extending usable calendar beyond pure beach swimming narratives.
