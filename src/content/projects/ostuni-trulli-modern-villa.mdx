## What Is the Ostuni Trulli Modern Villa?

A sixteenth-century four-cone trulli complex restored alongside a 200 sqm contemporary villa, on two hectares carrying more than 180 productive olive trees near Ostuni. Apulia Deluxe is the developer and the Soprintendenza heritage approvals are already secured. Pricing is on application, in the €800,000-1,500,000 range.

Apulia Deluxe specializes in heritage property development across Puglia with over 25 premium projects and recognition from Italian heritage authorities. This project showcases seamless integration of historical and modern elements on an olive estate 7 km from Ostuni centro and 40 km from Brindisi Airport.

## What Is the Cadastral Position on Trulli Conversions?

Cadastral regularisation is the recurring finding on trulli and the one foreign buyers most often discover late. Many structures predate formal permits and reach the market on a condono or conformità path that is not yet complete, which constrains both the works and the exit — and the professional fees to close it run €3,000-8,000.

| Item | Typical exposure |
|---|---|
| Cadastral regularisation | Commonly €3,000-€8,000 |
| Static engineer vault review | Commonly €2,000-€5,000 |
| Restoration capex | Often €800-€1,500 per sqm |
| Tourist use | CIN registration plus comune approval |

- Settle the title position before locking a restoration budget; an unresolved title limits works and resale alike.
- Commune stops at roof stage are the usual consequence of skipping the engineer review.
- Budget IMU levied at 0.86% to 1.06% of the cadastral value annually on a second home.
