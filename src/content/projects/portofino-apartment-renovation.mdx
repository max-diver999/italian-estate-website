## What Is Portofino Apartment Renovation Stock?

Portofino apartment stock is harbour-adjacent and headland units from €1,000,000, typically 60-100 sqm, inside what is probably Europe's tightest luxury residential envelope. Licensed short stays return 2-3% gross. American, British and Swiss buyers supply the resale depth; supply itself cannot expand.

Engel & Voelkers Liguria markets turnkey restored apartments and raw units needing 200,000 to 500,000 EUR MEP and terrace remediation behind park-compliant facades. Each village lane carries distinct terrace legality, parking deed clarity, and Parco di Portofino permit history that buyers must verify before trophy compromesso on harbour photography.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Everything in Portofino is measured from the harbour: 2-10 minutes on foot to the marina, 10-15 minutes to Santa Margherita Ligure for anything resembling normal services. Tender pickup logistics matter to the buyer profile more than parking does. The brand recognition is global and does not need explaining.

Harbour lanes command maximum premiums where restaurant and yacht logistics suit staffed short-stay operations. Headland contrada toward Castello Brown trades partial harbour views at 15 to 25 percent per sqm discounts versus full marina panorama on comparable 80 sqm restored stock.

Village supply remains ultra-thin with no meaningful new-build infill under landscape protection. Owners marketing 1,200 to 2,500 EUR nightly short-stay should document helicopter and private transfer partners for guest expectations on cliff-road and parking logistics during regatta weeks.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Layouts are compact: 60-100 sqm at €1,000,000-2,500,000 restored, or a raw unit at €800,000-1,500,000 plus €250,000-600,000 of works. The recurring technical problem is terrace waterproofing behind protected façades, and any visible exterior work takes 6-12 months of park approval.

Inventory mixes fully restored turnkey apartments with raw units needing MEP, bathrooms, and terrace remediation meeting park and insurance standards. Buyers should commission geometra and park authority pre-checks before compromesso because illegal terrace enclosures destroy resale value when discovered during buyer surveys.

## What Does Parco di Portofino Mean for a Renovation?

Protected-area rules decide the works programme here, not the builder's quote. Foreign buyers on a €1,000,000 renovation ticket are operating inside the Parco di Portofino, where visible external alteration on historic stock requires clearance that commonly adds 12 to 18 months to a timetable.

| Factor | Effect on the plan |
|---|---|
| Parco di Portofino rules | Constrain visible external works |
| Soprintendenza clearance | Required before permitted works start |
| Approval timeline | Commonly 12-18 months on protected palazzi |
| Conformità edilizia | Built layout must match the cadastral plan |

- Establish the approval position before the offer; "turnkey STR" marketing on protected stock is frequently premature.
- Price the carrying cost of the approval period into the acquisition, not just the works budget.
- Budget an IMU charge between 0.86% and 1.06% of cadastral value annually on a second home.

The Parco Naturale Regionale di Portofino governs the promontory, and its vincolo reaches further into a renovation than buyers expect. External alteration visible within the protected area — windows, shutters, render colour, terrace structures, external plant for heating or cooling — requires authorisation before a contractor can start, and the timeline runs in months. Where the building is historic, Soprintendenza clearance applies separately on top. On harbour-facing stock at €12,000-18,000 per sqm, that constrains the works that would justify the price, and it is the reason interior-only refurbishments dominate here. Two further checks belong alongside it: whether any mooring right is a registered concession rather than an informal arrangement, and whether the cadastral plan matches the built layout. Price the renovation from a survey with the vincolo scoped, not from a builder's estimate.
