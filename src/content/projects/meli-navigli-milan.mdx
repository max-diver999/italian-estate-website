## What Is Meli 26 Navigli Martesana?

Meli 26 is 22 Class A4 off-plan homes across 2,700 sqm on Milan's Naviglio Martesana, developed by Okam Italy with Studio Asti, starting construction Q2 2026 for Q1 2028 handover. Concierge and co-working amenities are included; pricing is on request, so comparisons run against Prandina 34 nearby.

[Okam Italy](/developers/okam-italy/) entered the Milan market in 2025 as the Italian arm of Okam Group, targeting underutilised urban sites along canals and former industrial corridors. Meli 26 continues Okam's Navigli Martesana cluster alongside Prandina 34 and Maciachini Urban Retreat with English-language sales materials for remote foreign buyers.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

The Martesana canal corridor runs east from Milan toward Gorgonzola: 25-35 minutes to Duomo by metro and bus, 45-55 to Malpensa, with the NAMA Martesana amphitheatre and cycle paths walkable. ## What Should a Navigli Buyer Check Before Reserving?

Navigli is a conversion market rather than a greenfield one, so foreign buyers are underwriting existing fabric. The recurring findings are conformità gaps between the built layout and the cadastral plan, and a condominium regolamento that forbids the tourist use on which a 4% to 5% gross case usually depends.

| Check | Consequence if skipped |
|---|---|
| Conformità edilizia | The gap transfers to the buyer at the rogito |
| Condominium regolamento on tourist use | Can forbid short lets the comune would allow |
| CIN registration | Required before the unit is advertised for tourist use |
| Canal frontage constraints | Landscape rules limit external alteration |

- Settle all 4 before the caparra; none can be fixed by renegotiating price afterwards.
- If short lets are blocked, model a 12-month furnished lease instead: lower gross, materially lower vacancy.
- Budget IMU, set between 0.86% and 1.06% of cadastral value by the comune annually, plus spese condominiali on converted stock.

Four checks precede a reservation on Martesana off-plan, and they concern the counterparty as much as the building. Identify which entity signs the compromesso — Okam, FRIMM or an SPV — and pull its visura camerale, because the penalty clause binds that company alone. Confirm the fideiussione covers 100% of the caparra rather than a capped share, and note which bank issues it. Check that milestone payments follow certified construction stages with an inspection right rather than calendar dates. And read the condominium regolamento in draft, since new Milan buildings increasingly prohibit tourist letting outright, which removes a strategy before you own the unit. Against Martesana comparables of €5,200-6,800 per sqm and a Q1 2028 handover, those four answers matter more than the finish tier the sales material leads with.
