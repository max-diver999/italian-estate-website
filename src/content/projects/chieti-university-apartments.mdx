## What Is Chieti University Apartment Stock?

Chieti stock is restored bilocale and monolocale from €165,000 near the Chieti-Pescara medical campus, on 55-80 sqm, returning 4.5-5.5% gross on furnished leases. It is the cheapest major-city entry in Abruzzo — Pescara on the coast starts €30,000 higher for the same size unit.

Local agency and Immobiliare.it seed portfolios list campus corridor stock with parking deeds, elevator-compliant condominiums, and turnkey MEP on converted palazzi. Each unit carries independent administrator spese history, lift certificate status, and conformità filings that buyers must verify before September intake marketing cycles begin.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Chieti is a hill town 15-25 minutes inland from the Pescara coast, with the medical faculty campus 5-15 minutes on foot or by bus and the Gran Sasso behind it. The trade is explicit: the cheapest entry of any Abruzzo city, in exchange for car dependency and no beach.

University corridor periphery trades Pescara Adriatic rent depth for value floor entry and predictable academic calendar marketing. Properties with documented parking and elevator access outperform walk-up palazzo upper floors that medical students reject after single inspection visits each September intake season.

| Pescara Adriatic coast | 15 to 25 min drive |
| Regional hospital | 10 to 20 min |
| Rome by car | 140 to 170 min |
| L'Aquila | 45 to 75 min |

Campus corridor bilocale at 172,000 EUR generating 790 EUR monthly equals 5.51 percent gross before IMU and cedolare secca at 21 percent on furnished leases. Hill approach value bands often sit 45 to 50 percent below Pescara city per sqm on Immobiliare 2026 references when parking solutions are documented in lease annexes.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Campus-corridor monolocale of 40-55 sqm run €165,000-195,000; restored bilocale of 65-80 sqm with parking €165,000-230,000; centro periphery palazzo €220,000-300,000. Pre-1980 shared boiler systems are common and need MEP upgrading, which is the single most underpriced item on this market.

Inventory mixes fully restored turnkey units with partial renovation stock requiring conformità filings on earthquake-zone towers. Buyers should commission engineer inspection before accepting marketed turnkey status on tickets syndicated without three-year administrator spese history attachments reviewed by avvocato before compromesso.

September and January intakes account for 70-80% of annual lease signings, so a unit vacant in February usually stays vacant until September. Resale runs 12-18 months.

Regional hospital fellows add twelve-month tenant depth beyond pure student turnover on elevator-compliant stock near hospital employment corridors.

Non-resident EU buyers dominate Chieti university corridor transactions because ticket sizes below 200,000 EUR suit cross-border cash deployment without Italian mortgage complexity. Rogito typically completes in 60 to 90 days when conformità and administrator packets are complete pre-compromesso.

## How Does Student Letting Shape a Chieti Purchase?

Student demand is what built much of Chieti's campus-corridor stock, and it typically moved faster than the paperwork behind it. Subdivisions carried out to create additional lettable rooms frequently outran the cadastral plan, which is why conformità is the recurring finding for foreign buyers on tickets around €165,000.

| Feature | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Room subdivisions | Often unrecorded on the plan |
| Academic letting cycle | List before the autumn intake or wait |
| Contract length | Shorter than a standard 4+4 lease |

Budget IMU of 0.86% to 1.06%, charged on cadastral value annually, plus the re-letting cost that higher student turnover brings each year.
