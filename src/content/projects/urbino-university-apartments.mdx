## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Urbino is a Renaissance hill town, with the campus 5-15 minutes on foot or by bus and the Ducal Palace 10-20 minutes' walk. Ancona is 60-75 minutes by car. The trade is explicit: no year-round hospital tenant base, but entry 30-40% below Assisi centro on comparable UNESCO branding.

Università di Urbino intake each September and January supports furnished twelve-month leases when parking deeds attach before remote signing from northern European fellowship programs. Hill approach value bands deliver lower tickets with car dependency versus campus corridor bilocale with documented garage solutions.

## Why Does One Month of the Year Decide This Ticket?

Urbino lets on the academic calendar and the window is narrow. Leases are signed before the autumn intake, so a unit listed four to six weeks late usually waits for the following cycle rather than letting at a discount — which turns a timing error into a full year of lost income on an €800-1,600 monthly rent.

- Market before the intake; the calendar, not the price, determines occupancy.
- Student contracts run shorter than a 4+4 lease, so build turnover into net yield.
- UNESCO centro rules constrain external alteration on historic fabric.

Expect IMU of 0.86% to 1.06% on cadastral value each year, alongside €800 to €1,600 of condominium charges.
