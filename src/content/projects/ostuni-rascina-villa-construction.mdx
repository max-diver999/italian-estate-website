## What Governs a Build on an Ostuni Rural Parcel?

Construction on rural land is governed by the parcel, not the design. Foreign buyers commissioning a villa near Ostuni are constrained by agricultural classification, buildable volume ratios and the olive-tree protections that apply across Puglia, all of which are settled before a single drawing is worth pricing.

| Constraint | Effect |
|---|---|
| Agricultural classification | Sets what may be built and at what volume |
| Protected olive trees | Restrict clearance and siting |
| Pool | Separate SCIA filing |
| Access and utilities | Frequently excluded from headline pricing |

- Have a commercialista and geometra confirm classification and buildable volume before offer.
- Rural builds commonly run 12 to 18 months from permit to handover, before fit-out.
- Budget an IMU charge between 0.86% and 1.06% of cadastral value annually once the build is registered.

Building on an Ostuni contrada parcel is governed by land classification before it is governed by design. The terreno agricolo carries a buildable envelope expressed as a ratio to land area, so a large parcel does not automatically permit a large house, and a 300 sqm villa may exhaust what the zoning allows and foreclose the annexe or pool house assumed for later. On top of that sit the pool SCIA as a separate filing, borehole authorisation where water is not mains-supplied, and an access track that on contrada plots is frequently private and shared without a registered right of way. Landscape rules apply where the site is visible from protected viewpoints. On a €650,000-900,000 custom build, a geometra should confirm the envelope, the residual buildable area and the cadastral category before the offer rather than after.
