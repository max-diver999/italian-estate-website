## What Is Porto Cervo Villa Stock?

Porto Cervo villa stock is consorzio-governed sea-view property from €1,500,000 across the marina village, Pevero Golf and the Romazzino contrada, on 250-600 sqm. Licensed summer weekly rental returns 2-4% gross. German and Swiss buyers give the market its depth; inland Arzachena stock sells for a fraction of the per-sqm price.

Engel & Voelkers and Gallura luxury networks market mixed new-build infill and 1970s-1990s renovation compounds with pool terraces, staff quarters, and optional dock access. Each listing carries distinct consorzio approval history, maritime concession status, and builder track record that buyers must verify before compromesso on trophy tickets.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Porto Cervo is the marina village on the Costa Smeralda's north coast: 5-15 minutes on foot to the harbour, 10-20 minutes to the Pevero Golf Club, 35-45 minutes to Olbia airport. Against inland consorzio stock at €4,500-6,500 per sqm, buyers here give up hill privacy for the ability to walk to a berth.

Regatta weeks and July-August marina social season concentrate buyer activity when villas include tender pickup logistics and documented parking for guest transfers on SS125 coastal road. Shoulder September sailing weeks support extended weekly rental calendars when operators market Nordic family itineraries before school terms resume.

## What Should Buyers Establish Before a Porto Cervo Reservation?

Consortium rules and season length govern this ticket more than price per sqm. Foreign buyers at €1,500,000 are entering a managed resort environment where architectural covenants, service charges and a season of roughly 3 months shape both the running cost and the exit.

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Consortium architectural rules | Constrain alteration and external finishes |
| Annual service charges | Materially higher than ordinary condominium spese |
| Season length | Roughly 3 months of genuine demand |
| Resale pool | Narrow, international, and season-sensitive |

- Request the consortium regulations and the current charge schedule before the caparra.
- Underwrite annual occupancy, then subtract IMU, service charges and 21% cedolare secca on a registered lease.
- Budget IMU levied at 0.86% to 1.06% of the cadastral value annually, with no relief on a second home.
