## What Decides a Siena Country Villa Purchase?

Pool filings and land classification decide this ticket more than the villa itself. Foreign buyers at €450,000 in the Siena countryside should confirm that the pool carries its SCIA, that extensions match the cadastral plan, and that the attached land does not restrict what can be built next.

| Check | Typical exposure |
|---|---|
| Pool SCIA | Remediation commonly €25,000-€60,000 |
| Extensions | Conformità gaps transfer to the buyer |
| Land classification | Agricultural parcels limit further building |
| Tourist use | CIN registration plus comune approval |

- Commission a geometra survey against the cadastral plan before the caparra.
- If agriturismo letting is the plan, sequence the licence before the fit-out spend.
- Budget IMU at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value annually on a second home.
