## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Typical stock is a restored two-bedroom of 70-95 sqm with lift access in the hospital corridor zones. Walk-up palazzi trade 15-20% below lift-equipped equivalents, and a parking deed is what separates a listing that markets to hospital and public-sector tenants from one that competes with every other walk-up. Furnishing packages aimed at twelve-month contracts matter more here than finish quality, because the tenant is arriving for a posting rather than a lifestyle.

- **Standard 2-bed:** €155,000-195,000 on restored elevator stock
- **Monforte premium:** €165,000-220,000 before furnishing
- **Walk-up discount:** 15-20% versus elevator-compliant comparables
- **Parking deed:** Material lease marketing premium when garage attaches

Earthquake zone building compliance matters on older condominiums reviewed with geometra before capex commitments on pre-1980 towers near hospital access roads.

## What Should a Campobasso Buyer Expect on Income and Exit?

Campobasso is an administrative market, and that shapes both halves of the case. Rent is steady and vacancy low because tenants are public-sector, hospital and university staff on twelve-month contracts, but the foreign resale pool is thin and an exit depends on domestic demand.

- Income: predictable, with rent that moves slowly in either direction.
- Exit: plan a hold well beyond five years; local demand sets the pace of a sale.
- Compliance: earthquake-zone rules apply to much of the older Molise stock.

A second home carries IMU of 0.86% to 1.06% on cadastral value, and condominium charges usually between €800 and €1,600.

### What does earthquake-zone compliance involve here?

Much of Molise's older stock predates current seismic standards, and compliance is a survey question rather than a paperwork one. A geometra should confirm the building's classification and any structural works already carried out, because remediation on pre-1980 stock can exceed the price advantage that drew a buyer to the region.

| Check | Why it matters in Molise |
|---|---|
| Seismic classification | Applies across the region |
| Structural works history | Establishes what has already been remediated |
| Conformità edilizia | The built layout must match the cadastral plan |

- Commission the survey before the caparra: findings here are not renegotiable afterwards.
- The same survey informs the resale, where domestic buyers ask the same questions.
