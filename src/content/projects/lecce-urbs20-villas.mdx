## What Should Buyers Confirm on Lecce New-Build Villas?

New-build delivery terms are where a €425,000 Salento villa ticket is won or lost. A foreign buyer should confirm what secures money paid before the deed and what the capitolato actually covers, because pool, landscaping and access are commonly priced and filed separately from the house.

| Item | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Fideiussione | Must cover 100% of sums paid before the deed |
| Postuma decennale | 10-year structural cover, issued at the rogito |
| Pool | Separate SCIA filing and separate cost |
| Handover | Commonly 12-18 months on rural new-build |

- Read the capitolato line by line; a render is not a specification.
- Non-residents pay IMU, set between 0.86% and 1.06% of cadastral value by the comune with no primary-residence relief.
- Short-let income is taxed at 21% on the first unit and 26% on the second.
