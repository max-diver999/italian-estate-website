## What Is Termoli Coast Apartment Stock?

Restored two-bedroom units from about €185,000 in Molise's Adriatic hub, inside the walled borgo or along the lungomare. Municipality averages ran about €1,861 per sqm on Immobiliare May 2026 data with rents near €9.67 per sqm monthly, supporting 4.5-6% gross on a blended short-let and long-lease calendar across an April-to-October season. It is the cheapest genuine Adriatic coastal entry north of Puglia, and well below Abruzzo's Pescara.

Termoli stretches along the Adriatic as Molise's principal coastal comune on A14 motorway corridors toward Pescara northbound and inland [Campobasso](/areas/campobasso/) about 45-60 minutes westbound, supporting summer STR marketing and long-term furnished leases without paying Abruzzo capoluogo registration tax bands on identical floor area.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Termoli is Molise's primary coastal resort: Adriatic beachfront condominiums within walking distance of the borgo antico, with hospital employment spilling over from Campobasso inland. Two-bedroom lift-served stock starts at €165,000 and returns 4.5-5.5% gross on furnished leases: a genuinely rare combination of beach and year-round tenant.

Termoli coast location typically means walled centro storico borgo within walking links to Adriatic beach access, lungomare residential corridors with sea-view premiums, fishing port character supporting food-tourism STR photography, A14 access toward Pescara 90-110 minutes northbound and [Campobasso](/areas/campobasso/) 45-60 minutes westbound, and Molise province premium positioning above inland comune about €1,085/m² that map for German and Dutch Adriatic enquiry.

- **Lungomare corridor:** Highest STR nightly rates; weekend coastal noise
- **Centro borgo:** Walled historic character; thinner elevator stock
- **Port district:** Fishing port tourism; mixed residential use
- **A14 motorway:** Primary Adriatic connectivity for guest arrival

Unlike inland [Campobasso centro apartments](/projects/campobasso-centro-apartments/) at about €155,000, Termoli trades higher acquisition basis for Adriatic rent bands and summer STR supplements.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?
