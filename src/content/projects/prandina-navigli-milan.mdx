## What Is Prandina 34 Navigli Martesana in Milan?

Prandina 34 is 30 Class A apartments across 3,500 sqm on Via Prandina in Milan's regenerating Martesana corridor, handing over Q2 2028. Okam Italy develops it with Albero Architecture and FRIMM construction, on milestone payments — Navigli identity without the full Darsena premium.

The project sits east of the famous Darsena nightlife strip but increasingly connected through cycling paths, bus upgrades, and planned metro extensions. Buyers gain canal-adjacent lifestyle associations with longer-lease tenant stability typical of Martesana addresses versus pure STR-focused Darsena stock.

## How Does Letting Regulation Shape the Prandina Case?

The Prandina yield case rests on furnished letting, and in Milan that requires 3 separate permissions to line up. Foreign buyers typically model a 4% to 5% gross short-let return without confirming that the comune, the CIN register and the condominium regolamento all permit the use in question.

| Permission | Who controls it |
|---|---|
| CIN registration | National register; required before advertising tourist use |
| Tourist-use rules | Comune of Milan, tightened in recent years |
| Regolamento condominiale | The building's own assembly, and it can override |

- Short-let income is taxed at 21% on the first unit and 26% on the second; from the third the activity is presumed a business.
- If short lets are unavailable, a 12-month furnished lease to a corporate or academic tenant is the fallback a lender will underwrite.
- Confirm the position for the specific address, not for Milan in general.
