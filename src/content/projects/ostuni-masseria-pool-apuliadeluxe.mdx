## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Stock is restored or newly built stone farm volumes with private pool terraces and four-bedroom layouts suited to group short-let; guest casitas appear on the €1,200,000-plus tiers. Mechanical, electrical and climate systems must meet habitability standards, which older masserie frequently do not. Verify the pool SCIA documentation independently rather than relying on the developer's permit marketing, since the pool is the single most commonly unauthorised element in Puglia.

- **Standard luxury:** €800,000 POA with masseria stone and pool compound
- **Ultra-premium:** €1,200,000-1,500,000 with casitas and commercial kitchens
- **Land scale:** Olive grove hectares vary materially across POA lots
- **Heritage:** Landscape vincolo may restrict pool placement by contrada

Pricing remains POA because land hectares, restoration depth, and guest annex volumes differ across lots. Buyers wanting UNESCO trulli cones should prioritize [Ostuni trulli modern villa](/projects/ostuni-trulli-modern-villa/) instead.

## What Should Foreign Buyers Verify Before Reserving?

Five checks before a price-on-application compromesso. The agricultural land category and buildable envelope, confirmed by a geometra; the pool SCIA and the short-let licensing path; the developer's permit package reviewed by independent counsel rather than accepted as presented; the restoration scope on stone and MEP; and IMU and cedolare secca modelled in full.

| Red flag | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Land category | Agricultural conversion and buildable envelope |
| Pool SCIA | Tourist rental licensing before yield assumptions |
| POA scope | Pool, walls, landscaping included versus extras |
| Sewage | Municipal connection versus septic on contrada |
| Reference estates | Completed handovers with STR track records |

## What Should Be Settled Before an €800,000 Masseria Reservation?

Permit status and land classification decide this purchase more than finish quality. Foreign buyers at €800,000 in the Ostuni countryside are typically acquiring a restored masseria on agricultural land, where the pool filing and the conformità of extensions must be clear before the caparra rather than after.

| Check | Why it binds |
|---|---|
| Pool SCIA | Remediation commonly runs €25,000-€60,000 |
| Extensions and outbuildings | Often built without matching permits |
| Agricultural classification | Limits further building on the parcel |
| Tourist use | Requires CIN registration and comune approval |

- Commission a geometra survey against the cadastral plan before the caparra, not after the deposit.
- Short-let income is taxed at 21% on the first unit and 26% on the second.
- Budget annual IMU running 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value annually, with no relief on a second home.
