## What Is MilanoSesto Urban Regeneration in Milan?

MilanoSesto is Italy's largest post-industrial regeneration: 1.4 million sqm of former Falck steelworks in Sesto San Giovanni, on a Foster + Partners masterplan, owned by COIMA SGR and Near SGR since 2023. The Unione Zero phase carries 480 free-market homes and over 600 student beds, on a 10-15 year horizon.

November 2023 agreements transferred MilanoSesto SpA from Hines to Italian institutional capital, relaunching delivery under advanced ESG standards. The masterplan spans residential, offices, Lombardy's City of Health campus, and a 45-hectare public park visible from Foster territorial diagrams on milanosesto.it.

## What Are the Key Facts for MilanoSesto?

MilanoSesto covers more than 1,400,000 sqm on the former Falck steelworks, with Unione Zero as the flagship at 250,000 sqm: 480 open-market apartments, more than 600 student beds, a 48,000 sqm Intesa Sanpaolo headquarters, and 350 social units reserved for hospital staff. Delivery is phased across decades. A foreign co-investor should model that horizon before committing to any fund or cooperative structure here.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Sesto San Giovanni sits on Milan's north-east edge: 20-30 minutes to Duomo by transit, with its own station on site, Malpensa 40-50 minutes away and the MIND district 15-25. Per-sqm pricing runs 15-30% below central Milan, and the tenant base is hospital staff and Intesa campus employees.

- **Employment anchor:** City of Health and Intesa Sanpaolo HQ create weekday demand
- **Transit:** New station links regional rail and metro toward Milan centro
- **Park:** 45 hectares public green backbone improves long-term livability scores
- **Competition:** Cascina Merlata and MIND supply 2026-2028 off-plan alternatives

Sesto trades lower per sqm than Navigli or Porta Nuova while sitting inside Milan metropolitan employment basin. Buyers from abroad should verify commute patterns to personal workplaces before choosing northeast regeneration over northwest M1 corridors.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Unione Zero's open-market apartments are by Scandurra Studio; more than 600 student beds occupy 39,000 sqm by Park Associati; 285 affordable units are by Barreca & La Varra. Title types differ across the scheme — cooperative in places, freehold in others — and foreign buyers cannot access any of the 350 hospital social tranches. Establish which category a specific unit falls into before treating a price as comparable.

| Component | Scale | Designer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free-market homes | 480 apartments | Scandurra Studio |
| Student housing | 600+ beds, 39,000 sqm GBA | Park Associati |
| Affordable housing | 285 apartments | Barreca & La Varra |
| Intesa Sanpaolo HQ | 48,000 sqm GBA | ACPV |
| Multi-tenant offices | 45,000 sqm GBA | ACPV |

COIMA and Near acquired 27,000 sqm for approximately 350 social apartments targeting City of Health employees. Remaining Unione Zero land (~128,000 sqm) stays under Prelios SGR Unione Zero fund with Cale Street and Hines coinvestors.

## What Should You Know About Investment Case?

The thesis is land appreciation tied to the City of Health completion and park activation over 10-15 years, not an off-plan flip. Student housing here competes with the Bicocca demand pool. Most foreign participation is fund coinvestment through the COIMA Housing Fund rather than direct freehold purchase.

| Factor | Benchmark |
| --- | --- |
| Hold horizon | 10-15 years multi-phase masterplan |
| PBSA scale | 600+ beds in Unione Zero alone |
| Social restriction | 350+ hospital units employee-only |
| Park uplift | 45 hectares green premium vs industrial stigma |
| Fund access | COIMA Housing Fund, Near Re-City mandates |

Long-term thesis rests on Foster masterplan prestige converting industrial legacy into northeast Milan lifestyle branding. PBSA on 600+ beds competes with [L'Innesto](/projects/innesto-milan-social-housing/) 300 beds and northwest towers. Construction noise on legacy resale may discount 8-12% until adjacent phases complete.

Foreign freehold buyers must identify which building markets units on public portals versus employee-only allocation. Social housing tranches follow Italian allocation law and restrict non-eligible foreign access on key volumes.

## Who Is This For?

This suits patient institutional capital rather than individual buyers. Coinvestors in COIMA Housing Fund or Near Re-City mandates; student housing operators who can work at the 600-bed Unione Zero scale; and north-east Milan mappers comparing it against Lendlease MIND on a 10-15 year phased delivery.

- **Fund coinvestors:** COIMA Housing Fund or Near Re-City regeneration exposure
- **PBSA operators:** 600+ beds competing with Bicocca student demand
- **Hospital staff:** 350 social units for City of Health employees (eligibility rules)
- **Land appreciation:** 10-15 year hold on Foster masterplan prestige

MilanoSesto suits patient capital and disqualifies almost everyone else, which is the honest framing for a site of more than 1,400,000 sqm. It fits an investor comfortable with fund or cooperative structures rather than direct freehold, since title types vary across the scheme and the 350 units reserved for hospital staff are closed to foreign buyers entirely. It fits a buyer taking the Unione Zero open-market tranche — 480 apartments alongside more than 600 student beds and a 48,000 sqm Intesa Sanpaolo headquarters — with a 10 to 15 year view. It does not fit anyone pricing this against a completed Milan apartment: the 45-hectare park, the retail and the transport upgrades all arrive behind the buildings, and legacy remediation on industrial soil sits underneath the programme.

## What Should You Know About Pros and Cons?

In favour: a Foster masterplan across 1.4 million sqm, 480 open-market homes, more than 600 student beds and a 45-hectare park. Against: hold horizons of 10-15 years before the district matures, 350 units restricted to hospital employees, legacy remediation on industrial soil, and fund-structure complexity that a direct purchaser is not used to pricing. Stress-test all four before wiring a deposit.

## What Should Foreign Buyers Verify Before Reserving?

Five items before any deposit. Whether the seller on a specific building is the fund or a freeholder; whether allocation is open-market or employee-only among the 350 social units; bonifica remediation status on the target lots inside the 1.4 million sqm footprint; COIMA Housing Fund disclosure; and avvocato review of any cooperative title.

| Red flag | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Seller entity | Fund escrow versus direct developer sale |
| Allocation | Employee-only on 350 hospital social units |
| Remediation | Bonifica status on target Falck lot |
| PBSA terms | 600+ bed operator occupancy standards |
| Phase timing | 8-12% noise discount on legacy resale nearby |

1. Verify seller entity and fund structure on compromesso header
2. Confirm open-market versus employee allocation on chosen typology
3. Cross-read [COIMA developer profile](/developers/coima/) and [Redo SGR](/developers/redo-sgr-italy/)
4. Compare PBSA operator terms against [L'Innesto](/projects/innesto-milan-social-housing/) demand data
5. Model 10-year hold horizon given multi-phase masterplan scope

**Insider tip:** recommend written confirmation of which Unione Zero buildings accept non-resident freehold before any wire to fund escrow accounts.

## What Is the Realistic Horizon at Sesto?

Scale sets the holding period at Sesto San Giovanni rather than the other way round. Foreign buyers entering during construction are underwriting a completed district that arrives in phases over 5 to 10 years, while today's comparable stock is still post-industrial Sesto at a materially lower price per sqm.

| Reference point | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| Entry comparison | Existing post-industrial Sesto stock |
| Exit comparison | Whatever has opened by year 5 to 7 |
| Fideiussione | Must cover 100% of sums paid before the deed |
| Postuma decennale | 10-year structural cover, issued at the rogito |

- Model the hold from your phase's certified completion date, not the reservation date.
- Treat the masterplan rendering as the exit case and current Sesto pricing as the entry case.
- Budget IMU of 0.86% to 1.06%, charged on cadastral value from handover on a second home.
