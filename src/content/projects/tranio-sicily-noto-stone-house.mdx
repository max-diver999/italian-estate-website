## What Should a €250,000 Noto Stone House Budget Cover?

Works and compliance, not purchase price, dominate this ticket. Foreign buyers at €250,000 in the Noto area are typically buying stone stock that requires structural, seismic and cadastral work before it can be occupied or let, and those lines routinely exceed the headline saving.

| Line | Typical band |
|---|---|
| Structural and seismic works | Site-specific; survey before offer |
| Cadastral regularisation | €3,000-€8,000 |
| Conversion conformità | €15,000-€35,000 on protected centro stock |
| Tourist use | CIN registration plus comune approval |

- Commission a geometra and structural survey before the caparra rather than after.
- South-eastern Sicily is a seismic zone, so compliance is a design constraint, not paperwork.
- Budget IMU, set between 0.86% and 1.06% of cadastral value by the comune annually on a second home.
