## What Is Ancona Centro Apartment Stock?

Ancona centro stock is restored bilocale in lift-served buildings from €190,000, in the Marche's Adriatic regional capital. Furnished long leases return 4.5-5.5% gross against an OMI 2026 reference near €1,541 per sqm. The tenant base is the point: hospital, port and university demand runs year-round rather than seasonally.

What separates one listing from another here is rarely the flat. It is the building: whether the lift certificate is current, whether three years of administrator spese can be produced, and whether a parking bay is deeded rather than informally used. A listing described as turnkey without those three documents is not yet turnkey.

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

Ancona is a working regional capital rather than a hill town, and that shapes the tenant base. Corso Mazzini is a 5-15 minute walk from centro stock, the regional hospital 10-20 minutes, the port 15-25. What a buyer gives up is UNESCO branding; what they get is a tenant in February at about €1,541 per sqm.

The letting year turns in September, when hospital appointments and the university intake land in the same fortnight. A flat ready then signs a twelve-month contract; one ready in November waits. Bologna is three to three and a half hours by car, and Ancona stock trades 15% to 25% below Bologna comparables for the same floor area, which is the argument a domestic reseller will reach for.

On a €200,000 budget the choice against [Urbino](/compare/ancona-vs-urbino-property/) comes down to photography against employment. Urbino has the walls and the name. Ancona has the hospital, the port and a tenant who renews. Parking decides the letting either way: hospital staff tend to rule out a flat without a bay on the first viewing and not come back.

## What Should You Know About Design and Units?

Stock is largely 1970s-1990s condominium: restored lift-served bilocale at €190,000-290,000, trilocale with parking at €270,000-340,000, and partially renovated units needing €15,000-40,000 of MEP work. On anything pre-1980 the shared boiler system usually needs a conformità filing, and that cost belongs in the offer.

Condition runs from genuinely finished, at APE class C or D, down to shells photographed well enough to look finished. An engineer settles which is which, not the listing, and on pre-1980 blocks the shared boiler is usually where it turns.

## What Should You Know About Investment Case?

At €900 to €1,050 a month, a furnished long lease on a bilocale bought between €190,000 and €240,000 returns 4.5% to 5.5% gross. Renewal pricing is set by the September intake, so a vacancy in October costs more than a vacancy in April. Resale runs 12-24 months, which makes this a cash-flow asset rather than a trading one.

Worked through: €198,000 buying €900 a month is €10,800 a year, or 5.45% gross, before IMU and cedolare secca at 21% on the first unit let. Condominium spese come off that, and so does any void between September cycles. The flat most likely to sit empty over a winter is the one without a parking bay.

Costs on a €200,000 second home land between 8% and 12% of the price, covering registration tax, the notaio and the engineer's inspection. An urban flat skips the land registry work that slows a rural purchase, so a rogito inside 60 to 90 days is realistic wherever conformità and the lift certificate are settled before the compromesso.

Ancona's investment case rests on a tenant who is there in February, which is rarer on the Adriatic than the coastline suggests. The regional hospital and the port supply salaried tenants on twelve-month contracts, and the university adds an academic-year layer on top, so a €190,000-220,000 bilocale with a parking deed models 4.5-5.5% gross with vacancy measured in weeks rather than months. OMI recorded urban growth of 5.89% year on year, which is real but should not carry an underwriting assumption on its own. What the buyer gives up is international recognition: this is a working port capital, not a hill town, and the resale market is almost entirely domestic. Hold it for the income across several cycles rather than for an appreciation story, and price the exit against local comparables.

## What Should Foreign Buyers Verify Before Reserving?

Five checks before compromesso on a €190,000-340,000 ticket. The elevator certificate and three years of condominium spese; the parking deed, which decides whether hospital tenants will look at all; a conformità audit on pre-1980 MEP; net yield modelled after IMU and cedolare secca; and independent avvocato review.

| Red flag | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Elevator gap | Certificate expiry and modernization vote status |
| Parking deed | Legal bays for hospital and port tenant leases |
| Conformità | Shared boiler and interior layout vs cadastral records |
| STR pro forma | LTR vs STR tax path with commercialista |
| Administrator spese | Three-year history before deposit |
| APE mismatch | Energy class vs utility-inclusive marketing |

Work in this order. Secure the codice fiscale and settle the notary-led wire path before the compromesso, because resale here moves quickly once a seller accepts. Put an engineer on the shared boiler and the MEP risers on anything pre-1980; that is where the money hides on this stock. Then take the tax path seriously rather than at portal face value: model the net after IMU and cedolare secca with a commercialista, using the [Italy rental yield guide](/guides/italy-rental-yield-guide/) for the deductions, and have encumbrances read by [your own avvocato](/guides/due-diligence-italy-property/) rather than the agency's.

Two comparisons are worth making before you commit capital. Read [Ancona against Urbino](/compare/ancona-vs-urbino-property/) if the allocation could go either way, and check the hospital corridor ticket you are looking at against the pricing bands in the [Ancona area guide](/areas/ancona/). The full order of operations for a foreign buyer, with the port corridor's effect on turnover added to the pre-caparra file, is in our [guide for foreign buyers](/guides/buy-property-italy-foreigner/).

Price this from sales that have completed in the same quartiere, and factor in what the port corridor does to tenant turnover. Spring listings run ahead of the OMI band, because hospital fellowship intake concentrates enquiry into a few weeks and asking prices follow the enquiry rather than the completions.

## Why Does Building Position Matter So Much in Ancona?

Ancona's stock splits between two corridors that behave nothing alike at broadly similar prices per sqm. Port-facing blocks carry freight noise and higher turnover; hospital-corridor buildings let steadily to salaried staff on twelve-month contracts at €900-1,800 a month. A foreign buyer's experience depends entirely on which corridor a listing sits in.

| Corridor | Tenant profile | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Port-facing | Transient, higher churn | Visit on a weekday at peak freight hours |
| Hospital corridor | Salaried, 12-month leases | Pre-1980 conformità gaps |

Carry it for a year and the bill is IMU, charged between 0.86% and 1.06% of cadastral value with no primary-residence relief on a second home, plus condominium charges that run €900 to €1,800 on buildings of this age.
