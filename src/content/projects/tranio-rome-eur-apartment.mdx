## What Are the Key Facts for Tranio EUR Stock?

Because each listing belongs to a different Rome promoter working an infill plot, there is no unified delivery programme of the kind [COIMA Guido Reni](/projects/coima-guido-reni-rome/) runs — completion dates, specification and guarantees vary between two apartments on the same street. On €400,000-500,000 tickets, confirm the underground parking allocation, the Class A energy certificate and the kitchen appliance tier before making an offer: corporate tenants in EUR reject units without secure car storage.

- **Developer model:** Independent promoters per building, Tranio as aggregator

- **Parking:** Underground allocation materially affects lease appeal

## What Should You Know About Location and Area?

EUR is Rome's planned business district south of the centro storico, built around the Cristoforo Colombo and Laurentina corridors, with multinational offices, the convention centre and a metro line that reaches the Colosseo in 20-30 minutes. It is the counterweight to [Rome centro storico](/areas/rome-centro-storico/): corporate lease pipelines instead of tourism short-let, on €350,000-450,000 deployments where predictable occupancy matters more than an address with a view.

## What Are the Running Costs of Owning in EUR?

Annual carry on an EUR apartment is the figure most non-resident budgets understate. On a €400,000 two-bedroom held as a second home, expect IMU of 0.86% to 1.06%, charged on cadastral value set by Roma Capitale, condominium charges of €1,800 to €3,000 a year on Class A stock with a concierge and lift, and rental tax of 21% on a registered long-term lease.

| Line | Annual band | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| IMU (second home) | 0.86%-1.06% of cadastral value | Set by Roma Capitale, no primary-residence relief |
| Spese condominiali | €1,800-€3,000 | Higher on Class A stock with concierge and lift |
| Cedolare secca on long lease | 21% of gross rent | No expense deduction under this regime |

Two EUR-specific points change the arithmetic. Class A buildings carry higher condominium charges than pre-1980 Rome stock, so the headline 3-5% gross on a corporate lease compresses faster here than in centro. And because the corporate tenant base signs twelve-month contracts rather than short lets, the CIN registration and tourist-use rules that dominate centro underwriting simply do not apply.
