## What Protects the Deposit Before Handover?

Off-plan purchases in Italy are governed by the developer-guarantee rules, and on a €348,500 Uptown ticket they are the single most important thing to check. The builder must issue a fideiussione covering every sum paid before the deed, plus a ten-year postuma decennale insurance policy on structural defects from handover.

- **Fideiussione:** must cover the full amount paid to date, not the deposit alone, and be issued by a bank or insurer, not the developer.
- **Postuma decennale:** ten-year structural cover, handed over at the rogito rather than promised in the brochure.
- **Milestone schedule:** payments should attach to certified construction stages, so a slipped stage delays your money as well as the keys.

Cascina Merlata is a phased masterplan, so ask which phase your unit sits in and what completed around it. A unit in an early phase is delivered into a working district; a unit in a late phase is delivered into a building site with the amenity pricing already in the ticket.
