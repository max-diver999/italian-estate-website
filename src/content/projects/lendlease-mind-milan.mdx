## What Is the Tenant Case at MIND?

MIND is an innovation district built around research, hospital and university occupiers, which gives a €380,000 apartment there an unusual tenant profile for Milan. The demand is institutional and salaried rather than tourist, so the underwriting looks more like a long-let case than a short-let one.

- **Tenant base:** researchers, clinicians and postgraduate staff on fixed-term contracts, which suits twelve-month furnished leases.
- **Vacancy:** lower than tourism-led Milan stock, but tied to the district's occupier build-out rather than to citywide demand.
- **Phasing:** confirm which buildings and which occupiers are actually operating on your handover date.

The risk is concentration. A district whose demand comes from a small number of large institutions is exposed to their decisions in a way that a diversified city-centre location is not.
