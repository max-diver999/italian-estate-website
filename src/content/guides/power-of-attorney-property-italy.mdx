## What is a Procura Speciale (Special Power of Attorney)?

A Procura Speciale is a limited power of attorney that authorizes one named representative to sign compromesso and rogito for a single Italian property identified by foglio, particella, subalterno data, and a capped euro price in 2026 remote files for foreign buyers who cannot attend notaio in person under Italian deed rules.

Remote files budget 500 to 1,500 EUR drafting, 100 to 300 EUR home notarization, and 10 to 20 percent deposit exposure if cadastral pins are wrong at rogito week.

The Italian Procura Speciale serves as the primary mechanism for international investors who wish to [buy property in Italy as a foreigner](/guides/buy-property-italy-foreigner/) without travel.

- Limit authority to one transaction near 500 to 1,500 EUR drafting cost
- Cap purchase price in text so proxy cannot exceed agreed euro amount
- Attach 10 to 20 percent deposit protections via independent avvocato review
- Use the procura buyer scenario checklist before shipping originals

This legal instrument empowers a trusted proxy to execute specific actions, including signing the preliminary purchase contract (contratto preliminare) and concluding the final deed of sale before an Italian notary.

| Element | Requirement | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Principal identity | Passport plus codice fiscale | Notaio verification |
| Property pin | Foglio, particella, subalterno | Prevents wrong unit purchase |
| Price cap | Maximum authorized euro amount | Protects buyer deposit |
| Language | Italian or bilingual | Notaio acceptance |

- Sign only after cadastral data matches compromesso
- Limit powers to the single transaction described
- Attach Apostille or consulate legalization before shipping to Italy

When foreign buyers decide to acquire real estate in Italy, the Italian legal system requires precise documentation to verify the identity and intent of all contracting parties. If the power of attorney is drafted incorrectly, the public notary will refuse to execute the final sale deed, which can lead to costly delays and potential breaches of the preliminary contract.

Insider tip: Never grant Procura Generale when a single Procura Speciale covers one rogito; excess authority creates red flag exposure if the proxy relationship breaks before closing.

The Procura Speciale is almost universally preferred for property transactions because this document minimizes the risk of abuse by restricting the representative's authority to a single, well-defined event.

## How do you draft and format an Italian power of attorney?

A procura has to be drafted in Italian, or bilingually, and it has to be specific. It names the exact cadastral foglio and particella, sets a maximum purchase price, and states explicitly whether mortgage signing is authorised. A notaio will not accept a general authority for a property transaction.

Because the Italian legal system is highly formalistic, the Procura Speciale must identify the exact property being purchased with dati catastali embedded in the deed text.

- Copy foglio, particella, and subalterno from visura catastale, not brochures
- State maximum euro price and 10 to 20 percent deposit authorization limits
- Name Intesa, UniCredit, or other bank if mortgage signing is included
- Budget 500 to 1,500 EUR lawyer drafting plus 100 to 300 EUR home notary fees

| Drafting Component | Required Information | Legal Purpose / Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Principal's Details** | Full name, date and place of birth, residential address, tax code (*codice fiscale*) | Establishes the legal identity of the foreign buyer granting the power |
| **Representative's Details** | Full name, date and place of birth, professional address, professional ID | Identifies the proxy who is authorized to sign the deeds in Italy |
| **Property Cadastral Data** | *Foglio* (sheet), *particella* (parcel), *subalterno* (subordinate) numbers | Pinpoints the exact real estate unit to prevent unauthorized purchases |
| **Transaction Boundaries** | Maximum purchase price, agreed payment methods, financing details | Limits the representative's financial authority to protect the buyer |

- Never use generic internet templates for Italian notaio files
- Copy cadastral numbers from visura catastale, not marketing brochures
- Specify banking institutions if mortgage signing is authorized

## How do you legalize a power of attorney signed abroad?

Legalisation depends on whether the signing country is party to the Hague Convention. If it is, the document is notarised locally, an Apostille is obtained — anywhere from fifteen minutes to fourteen days — and a certified Italian translation attached. If not, Ministry and consular steps add roughly four weeks.

The legalization process represents the most critical administrative step in preparing a Procura Speciale outside of Italy.

- US, UK, and Australia use Apostille after local notary signing
- UAE and China need consular legalization up to 4 weeks longer
- Budget 100 to 300 EUR home notary fee before Apostille queue

For citizens residing in countries that signed the Hague Convention of 1961, such as the United States, the United Kingdom, or Australia, the legalization process is relatively straightforward and relies on the Apostille stamp.

| Step | Hague Convention Countries (e.g., US, UK, Canada) | Non-Hague Convention Countries (e.g., UAE, China) |
|---|---|---|
| **Step 1: Drafting** | Lawyer drafts bilingual (Italian/English) *Procura Speciale* | Lawyer drafts bilingual (Italian/English) *Procura Speciale* |
| **Step 2: Signing** | Principal signs before a local notary public in the home country | Principal signs before a local notary public in the home country |
| **Step 3: Certification** | State or national authority applies the international **Apostille** | Ministry of Foreign Affairs certifies the local notary's signature |
| **Step 4: Legalization** | No further action required; the Apostille is fully recognized | Nearest **Italian Consulate** legalizes the document for use in Italy |
| **Step 5: Translation** | Certified translation into Italian is verified by an Italian court | Certified translation into Italian is verified by the consulate |

- Start legalization the week seller accepts your offer
- Ship originals by tracked courier to Italian lawyer before rogito date
- Budget extra weeks for consular backlogs outside Hague countries

Appoint an independent bilingual avvocato carrying professional liability cover, and specifically not the selling agent. The conflict is not theoretical: the same person collecting a commission on the sale would be signing away a 10-20% compromesso deposit on the buyer's behalf, with no realistic recourse afterwards.

Typical avvocato proxy fees run 500 to 1,500 EUR per transaction, while agent conflicts have triggered 10 to 20 percent deposit disputes when dual agency was undisclosed.

While Italian law allows property buyers to appoint any capable adult as their proxy, selecting the correct representative is essential for safeguarding the transaction.

- Prefer avvocato with indemnity insurance over commission-driven agent
- Reject dual agency where one agent represents seller and buyer
- Use representative red flag checklist before granting signature power

Appointing a real estate agent creates an immediate conflict of interest because the agent's primary financial motivation is to close the sale and collect the commission.

- Verify professional indemnity insurance before granting signature power
- Ensure representative attends notaio meetings with full document set
- Refuse dual agency where agent represents seller and buyer

An independent, bilingual real estate lawyer acts as a fiduciary, bound by strict professional codes of conduct and comprehensive professional liability insurance. ## How do you revoke a power of attorney in Italy?

Revocation is a formal deed executed through an Italian notaio or a consulate. The previous representative must then be notified by registered post or PEC, and a copy filed with the transaction notaio before any replacement proxy signs. Skipping the notification step is what leaves an old authority live.

Insider tip: Attach revocation to the notaio file before scheduled rogito; stale procura signatures void deeds and delay registration by 2 to 4 weeks.

While a Procura Speciale naturally expires upon the successful registration of the final deed of sale, buyers must understand the mechanisms for early termination.

- Draft bilingual revocation deed matching original legalization path
- Send PEC or registered mail to old proxy same week as notaio notice
- Attach revocation to file before scheduled rogito to block stale signatures

If a buyer decides to change representatives during the purchase process, the buyer must execute a formal, written revocation to legally void the existing power of attorney.

| Stage | Action | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Draft revocation | Lawyer prepares bilingual deed | Before new proxy acts |
| Legalize revocation | Same Apostille or consular path | Match original procura |
| Notify old proxy | Registered mail or PEC | Same week as legalization |
| Inform notaio | Attach revocation to file | Before scheduled rogito |

- Limited Procura Speciale reduces damage if relationships sour
- Keep timestamps on all revocation deliveries
- Confirm notaio archived revocation before deed signing
