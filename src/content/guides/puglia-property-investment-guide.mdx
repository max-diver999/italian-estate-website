Puglia legal compliance requires trullo restoration permits averaging 4 to 8 months, mandatory CIN codes with fines to €8,000, and olive-tree protection checks on rural tickets above 2 hectares for foreign buyers. Water-rights verification on wells is essential before any 10% compromesso deposit on €250,000 masserie purchases.

Puglia property investment involves several unique legal requirements beyond standard Italian property law, particularly for trulli and rural properties.

**Checklist:** Verify CIN eligibility, SCIA quotas, olive-tree certificates, and well rights before paying a 10% deposit on rural trulli.
