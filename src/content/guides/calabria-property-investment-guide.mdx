## Which Calabria comuni qualify for the 7% pension flat tax regime?

The 7% pension flat tax regime in Calabria typically means Article 24-ter TUIR substitute tax on foreign-source income for up to nine years in comuni with ISTAT population at or under 30,000 residents from 7 April 2026 under Law 34/2026, per MORE Group pension relocation screening on Tropea and Scalea tickets.

Reggio Calabria and Cosenza city exceed the threshold and do not qualify at municipal level.

- Italian rental income from Calabria property stays on cedolare secca separately from foreign pension election
