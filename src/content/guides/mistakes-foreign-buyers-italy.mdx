## What is abusivismo edilizio and why does it kill deals?

Abusivismo edilizio is unauthorized construction or renovation without valid concessione edilizia or SCIA, and it affects an estimated 15% to 25% of tourist-region listings offered to foreign buyers. Fines start at €516 per square meter of illegal work and can void a notarial sale on properties above €250,000.

| Violation scale | Examples | Typical exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Interior moves, enclosed terraces | €5,000-25,000 regularization |
| Major | Extra floors, use changes | Demolition order risk |
| Detection | Survey vs permit set | 10-20 business days |

- Commission geometra comparison of built sqm to approved plans before compromesso.
- Obtain certificato di conformità or pending regularization timeline in writing from seller.
- **Red flag:** Pool, extension, or loft conversion with no permit reference in visura urbanistica file.

## Why must buyers never skip the visura catastale?

Visura catastale is the official cadastral extract showing boundaries, category, and registered sqm, and it typically costs €20 to €50 online while preventing €10,000 to €30,000 boundary or tax reclassification disputes. Skipping it means buying assets that may not match land-registry maps used at rogito within 60 to 90 days of closing.

| Data field | Why it matters | Failure cost |
|---|---|---|
| Cadastral map | Boundary proof | €15,000+ neighbor litigation |
| Category A/10 | Tax and use | IMU and use penalties |
| Registered sqm | Price per sqm | 8% to 12% overpayment |

- Cross-check visura with visura ipotecaria and on-site laser survey within 20 days of offer.
- Resolve agricultural vs residential class mismatches before caparra confirmatoria transfer.
- **Checklist:** Cadastral foglio, particella, and subalterno must match seller marketing PDFs.

## What CIN mistakes do STR buyers make after purchase?

CIN (Codice Identificativo Nazionale) is the national short-term rental ID, and it typically requires a new application within 2 to 8 weeks at €50 to €200 municipal cost rather than automatic transfer on a €250,000 to €600,000 coastal flat. Caps in Rome Zone A or Florence can block new codes entirely.

| Step | Timeline | Cost range |
|---|---|---|
| Verify seller CIN | Pre-compromesso | €0-150 admin copy |
| New owner application | 2-8 weeks | €50-200 |
| Revenue gap if denied | 3-12 months | €8,000-40,000 gross |

- Include CIN validity and transfer assistance as a suspensive clause where law allows.
- Read condominium regolamento for STR bans before paying 10% deposit.
- Model 21% cedolare secca on first home STR vs 26% on a second property let.
- **Red flag:** Listing shows CIN in photos but comune portal shows expired or seller-only operator status.

## How do buyers misuse prima casa tax benefits?

Prima casa is the primary-residence transfer-tax regime that allows 2% imposta instead of 9% for eligible Italian residents, but it requires tax residency within 18 months and five years of main-home use on purchases commonly priced €200,000 to €800,000. Misuse triggers repayment plus penalties from 30% to 120% on the tax difference.

| Regime | Transfer tax rate | Residency rule |
|---|---|---|
| Prima casa | 2% on deed value | Reside within 18 months |
| Second home | 9% for residents | No residency discount |
| Non-resident | 9% to 10% | No prima casa claim |

- Non-residents should budget 9% transfer tax on €400,000 purchases (€36,000) without prima casa assumptions.
- Register anagrafe and utility contracts only when genuine relocation is planned within 18 months.
- **Red flag:** Agent promises “2% tax” to a UK or US buyer with no Italian domicile plan.

## What do olive tree protection laws require from buyers?

Olive tree protection laws are regional rules that block removal of trees over 50 years old without agricultural authority permits, common in Puglia, Sicily, and Calabria on land parcels from 5,000 to 50,000 sqm. Fines range from €500 to €5,000 per tree plus mandatory replanting ratios up to 1:3.

| Region | Buffer / rule | Fine band |
|---|---|---|
| Puglia | Permit over 50-year trees | €500-5,000 per tree |
| Sicily | 50 m build buffer on heritage groves | €1,000-5,000 |
| Calabria | Root-zone disturbance permit | €500-4,000 |

- Commission agronomist inventory before renovation plans on masserie with 20+ trees.
- Map construction setbacks to protected root zones at 1:500 scale before pool or extension design.
- **Checklist:** Count trees over 50 years and verify Puglia or Sicily permit paths before land-price negotiation.

## What trullo permit issues trap foreign buyers in Puglia?

Trullo heritage compliance requires soprintendenza-approved materials and limits structural changes on cone roofs across Val d'Itria listings priced €300,000 to €1,200,000. Retroactive permits often cost €15,000 to €50,000 and reversing illegal mods runs €25,000 to €75,000 over 12 to 24 months on typical 150 sqm to 400 sqm complexes.

| Status | Permit path | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|
| UNESCO buffer (Alberobello) | Full heritage dossier | €20,000-50,000 |
| Regional trullo | Material-matched SCIA | €15,000-35,000 |
| Unauthorized bathroom insert | Regularize or remove | €10,000-40,000 |

- Require heritage architect letter confirming conformity before 10% compromesso on any trullo with post-1990 works.
- **Case study:** Buyers who cleared sale without cone-roof permits faced 14-month regularization and €32,000 fees in 2025 Val d'Itria files.

## What utility assumptions fail on rural Italian properties?

Rural utility assumptions are expectations that grid water, gas, and 6 kW power exist on countryside masserie where connections often require €5,000 to €15,000 electrical upgrades and €3,000 to €10,000 propane or gas line extensions within 500 m of mains on €350,000 to €900,000 rural deeds.

| Utility | Rural gap | Install range |
|---|---|---|
| Electricity | 3 kW vs 6 kW need | €2,000-8,000 upgrade |
| Water | Well-only supply | €5,000-12,000 new connection |
| Gas | No mains | €3,000-10,000 LPG system |

- Survey peak summer water flow and septic capacity before closing on pool or guest-suit plans.
- **Checklist:** Request ENEL or local DSO capacity letter and acqueduct entitlement before rogito.

## What ownership structure mistakes do foreigners make?

Ownership structure mistakes are choices such as prima casa-eligible personal title for pure STR portfolios or missing co-owner accords on €400,000+ family purchases, which raises IMU, registration, and exit tax friction by 3% to 8% of annual yield over a 5-year hold on two or more letting units.

| Structure | Best for | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | Personal use + one STR | Prima casa residency rules |
| SRL / company | Multi-unit rental | 24%+ corporate compliance |
| Co-ownership | Family holiday home | Missing exit accords |

- Draft bilingual co-owner use schedule and sale-trigger clauses before notary rogito on shared deeds.
- Model 26% withholding on non-resident rental vs 21% cedolare secca on individual first-home let.
- **Red flag:** Corporate wrapper promised for tax savings without 24%+ compliance budget on two or more units.
