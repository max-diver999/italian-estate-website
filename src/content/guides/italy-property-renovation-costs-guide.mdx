## What Do Renovation Costs per Square Metre Run in Italy in 2026?

Renovation costs in Italy run €400 to €2,500 per sqm in 2026, split into four bands by scope rather than by listing photos. Foreign buyers should price the band before the offer: a cosmetic refresh starts at €400/sqm, while trulli and masserie with engineer-led structural scope reach €2,500/sqm.

What sits inside each band: a cosmetic refresh covers paint, floors, a kitchen swap and bathroom retile on an already compliant apartment. A full refurbishment adds MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing), partitions, insulation and mid-spec finishes at €800-1,400/sqm. Heritage and stone farmhouse restoration with approved materials runs €1,200-2,000/sqm.


Use this three-question checklist, since all three are answerable before you offer:

- Is the electrical and plumbing original, or replaced in the last 15 years
- Does the roof structure tie into the walls, or sit on unconnected trusses
- Do the cadastral plans match the building as it stands today

These bands assume lawful starting points. Properties with [abusivismo](/guides/due-diligence-italy-property/) or missing conformità can double totals when sanatoria and demolition orders enter the SCIA path.

| Renovation band | €/sqm (2026 indicative) | Typical property | What is included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cosmetic refresh | €400-700 | Urban apartment, habitable shell | Surfaces, kitchens, baths, paint |
| Full refurb | €800-1,400 | Country house, villa, townhome | MEP, windows, insulation, layout |
| Heritage / stone restoration | €1,200-2,000 | Casale, centro storico, vincolato | Structural repair, lime, approved joinery |
| Complex / trullo / masseria | €1,800-2,500 | Multi-volume rural, cone clusters | Engineering, roofs, extensions, high spec |

IVA sits 10% on residential renovation for many primary-home classifications and 22% on luxury finishes or non-residential use, but foreign second-home buyers almost always pay the higher rate on contractor invoices unless Italian tax residency and prima casa criteria are met. Quotes marked "senza IVA" are standard: add 22% mentally before comparing to your sterling or dollar budget.

## How Long Do Italian Property Renovations Take?

Italian renovation timelines run 3 to 5 months for apartment cosmetics under CILA, 12 to 18 months for a full refurbishment with SCIA and new MEP, and 18 to 24 months where heritage, seismic work, or a pool is involved. Foreign buyers should plan around the longest band that applies.

A red flag in agency material is a six-month estimate attached to a project that clearly needs SCIA.


| Phase | Duration | What slows it down |
|---|---|---|
| Design + preventivo | 4-8 weeks | Multiple imprese bids, engineer surveys |
| Comune / CILA / SCIA | 6-16 weeks | Soprintendenza queue, SCIA alternativa |
| Structural + roof | 8-20 weeks | Weather, crane access on hillsides |
| MEP + plaster + floors | 12-24 weeks | Supply chains, artisan scheduling |
| Snagging + habitability | 4-8 weeks | APE certificate, fire norms, STR CIN prep |

Three scheduling rules protect a foreign owner calendar:

- Book the geometra before the impresa, since filings gate the start date
- Treat winter as design time in Apennine and inland comuni
- Fix the snagging visit in the contract, not after the final payment

Remote owners who visit quarterly should assume one full season lost to permits before interior work starts. [Tuscany](/guides/tuscany-property-investment-guide/) and [Puglia](/guides/puglia-property-investment-guide/) imprese often book six months ahead for spring start slots; signing in November may mean work begins the following April.

## How Much Does Seismic Retrofit Cost on Italian Property?

Seismic retrofit costs €200 to €450 per sqm on unreinforced masonry, and engineer-led projects on two-storey village houses land at €15,000 to €60,000 before finishes. The trigger is NTC 2018 zone classification plus any comune requirement for structural proof before a change of use, which catches foreign buyers converting to rentals.

Unreinforced masonry farmhouses in Abruzzo, Molise, Umbria, Marche, and the central Apennines carry the widest range, because ring beams, wall ties, roof diaphragms, and foundation stitching are often needed together.


| Element | Indicative cost | When required |
|---|---|---|
| Structural engineer model | €2,500-6,000 | Pre-1980 masonry, zone 2-3 |
| Ring beam + wall ties | €200-350/sqm | SCIA on structural changes |
| Foundation remediation | €15,000-40,000 | Hillside slip or inadequate footings |
| Roof diaphragm upgrade | €8,000-25,000 | Trusses not connected to walls |
| Post-earthquake compliance order | Variable | Inherited liability with deed |

A red flag on any pre-1975 stone building: a listing that mentions "recently restored" with no structural engineer report attached. Ask for these three before the compromesso:

- NTC 2018 zone classification for the comune
- Engineer opinion on wall ties and roof connection
- Any outstanding post-earthquake compliance order attached to the deed

Tax incentive note: Sismabonus and superbonus-era offsets have largely expired or narrowed for foreign non-resident buyers. Underwrite retrofit at full gross cost in 2026 models; do not count on refunds that require Italian tax residency and multi-year filings.

Properties in L'Aquila province or Molise hill towns marketed as "characterful" often need engineer reports before banks release renovation loans. Budget the survey before compromesso on any pre-1975 stone building.

## How Do UNESCO and Soprintendenza Rules Affect Renovation Costs?

UNESCO World Heritage buffers and Soprintendenza vincoli under D.Lgs 42/2004 are the largest single cost escalator after structural collapse. They do not block purchase; they constrain materials, timelines, and external volumes. Expect a 25-45% premium and 4-9 months of added authorization time on facade, roof, and window work, which foreign buyers rarely price in.


| Constraint type | Cost impact | Example geography |
|---|---|---|
| Bene culturale listing | +30-45% finishes | Historic palazzi, rural chapels converted |
| Vincolo paesaggistico | +15-30%, pool risk | Coastal bands, ridge lines |
| UNESCO buffer | Facade color, volume caps | Val d'Orcia, Cinque Terre hinterland |
| Archaeological monitor | €5,000-20,000 | Ground disturbance near known sites |

Buyer scenario worth pricing: a kitchen extension that breaks a roof cone profile, or a terrace within 150 m of a protected view, turns "internal work only" into a full Soprintendenza authorization with PVC windows rejected and wood sections mandated at triple unit cost.

A kitchen extension that breaks the roof cone profile or a terrace within 150 m of a protected view can trigger full Soprintendenza autorizzazione with rejected PVC windows and mandated wood sections at triple unit cost.

Before you accept "just internal work" as an answer, confirm:

- Whether the building carries a bene culturale listing or only a vincolo paesaggistico
- Whether the roof profile, facade colour, and window sections are prescribed
- Whether ground disturbance near a known site triggers archaeological monitoring at €5,000-20,000

Professional fees rise with heritage: specialist architects charge €150-250/hour in Florence or Lecce heritage practices versus €80-120/hour for standard residential CILA work in provincial towns.

Infinity edges, stone paving and heat pumps push the total toward €90,000, and hillside excavation with retaining walls can exceed €100,000 before foreign buyers reach the landscape permit.

Pool budgets are the capex line buyers defer to year two, which is when the vincolo paesaggistico question arrives.

Puglia and Tuscany rural listings market "pool-ready plot" without mains capacity. Upgrading electrical from 3 kW to 6-10 kW costs €3,000-8,000. Sewage and greywater compliance adds €5,000-15,000 if the pool backwash cannot tie to an approved system.

Pool timelines: 3-5 months build after permits, but permits in coastal Liguria or UNESCO-adjacent hills add 3-6 months. Combine pool SCIA with house SCIA where possible to avoid paying duplicate geometra filings.

## What Does Trullo Restoration Cost in Puglia?

Trullo restoration sits in its own cost bracket because cones are structural, not decorative. A single cone with sound stone and basic MEP runs €800-1,200/sqm. Multi-cone complexes run €1,400-2,200/sqm in the [Puglia market](/guides/puglia-property-investment-guide/), where cone count rather than floor area drives what foreign buyers pay.

Lamia barns, guest volumes, and high-spec kitchens sit at the top of that range, and Ostuni and Locorotondo keep labour competitive against Tuscany.


| Trullo project type | €/sqm range | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Single cone, holiday use | €800-1,200 | 12-16 months |
| Multi-cone + lamia | €1,200-1,800 | 16-22 months |
| Complex + pool + landscape | €1,800-2,500+ | 18-24 months |

Run this checklist on any cone before the deposit:

- Chianca re-chalking and calotte waterproofing, quoted as separate lines
- Lintels on every modified opening
- Insulation detailing hidden inside the stone
- A trulli-experienced engineer, at €3,000-7,000, rather than a generic residential one

Critical line items beyond sqm math: cone re-chalking (chianca), waterproofing the calotte, lintels on modified openings, and insulation hidden inside stone. Engineers familiar with trulli charge €3,000-7,000 for structural assessment; generic residential engineers miss cone creep and cost you a season.

Verify classificazione catastale and past amnesty filings before deposit. A trullo sold as "restored" may only have cosmetic lime wash while cones leak every winter.

## What Contractor Risks Inflate Italian Renovation Budgets?

Contractor risk is the silent multiplier that foreign buyers underestimate, and it usually arrives as 15-30% of change orders rather than as one dramatic failure. Italian construction is relationship-driven, so cold email chains produce fantasy preventivi, and a quote marked senza IVA hides another 22% on top of it.

| Red flag | Typical overrun | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Quote senza IVA, senza permits | +22% + €5k-15k fees | Capitolato with explicit scope |
| No partita IVA / DURC | Project stall, no warranty | Verify on Camera di Commercio |
| 100% cash, no milestones | Total loss risk | Staged payments tied to SAL reports |
| Single impresa for struct + MEP + pool | +15-30% change orders | Split trades, independent PM |
| "Friends of vendor" impresa | Hidden kickbacks, scope gaps | Three independent bids |

Four controls carry most of the protection:

- Three independent bids, none of them introduced by the vendor
- Partita IVA and DURC verified at the Camera di Commercio
- Staged payments tied to SAL progress reports, never 100% cash
- A direttore lavori at €800-1,500/month or 8-12% of works

Fixed-price a corpo contracts help only when the capitolato lists square metres, brands, permit responsibility, and contingency ownership. A misura (time and materials) suits unknown stone shells but needs weekly site reports.

Independent project manager (direttore lavori) fees of €800-1,500/month or 8-12% of works reduce foreign-owner overruns by catching unauthorized variations and unsigned SCIA starts. Starting build without posted SCIA exposes you to stop-work fines and insurance voids.

Language gap cost: hiring bilingual capocantiere adds €100-200/day but prevents the wrong tile order that delays a bath by six weeks.

## Which Permits and Professional Fees Add to Renovation Totals?

Permits and professional fees are not optional line items: the comune will not upgrade power or sign habitability without the correct filing. Budget €800 to €2,500 for a geometra CILA, €3,000 to €8,000 for an architect SCIA, and €2,000 to €10,000 where Soprintendenza clearance applies. Foreign buyers should assume the higher end on heritage stock.

| Service | Fee range | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Geometra CILA | €800-2,500 | Minor works filing |
| Architect SCIA | €3,000-8,000 | Structural, layout changes |
| Structural engineer | €2,500-7,000 | Seismic, openings, loads |
| Soprintendenza filing | €2,000-10,000 | Heritage authorization |
| APE (energy cert) | €150-400 | Mandatory on sale/rent |
| CIN / STR compliance | €500-2,000 | Tourist rental registration |

Match the filing to the work before you price it:

- CILA: internal, non-structural updates
- SCIA: structural changes, facades, volume shifts
- Permesso di costruire: extensions and new volumes
- Soprintendenza first: any heritage property, before the comune will even accept the file

CILA suits internal non-structural updates. SCIA covers structural changes, facades, and volume shifts. Permesso di costruire applies to extensions and new volumes. Heritage properties need Soprintendenza clearance before comune acceptance, detailed in our [heritage guide](/guides/heritage-restricted-property-italy/).

A red flag at filing stage: demolition starting before the SCIA is posted on site, which voids insurance and invites a stop-work fine. Utility connections on rural Abruzzo/Molise plots: water drill or cistern €8,000-20,000, septic to code €6,000-15,000, grid upgrade €3,000-10,000. Urban apartments may only need condominium spese straordinarie for elevator or facade projects you inherit at closing.

Three adjustments apply on top of the base model:

- Pool inside a vincolo paesaggistico: landscape filings at €5,000-15,000 in specialist fees alone
- Soprintendenza involvement: 4 to 9 months before the first approved scaffold
- Southern regions: 15-25% lower quotes, offset by longer timelines

Calendar from rogito to habitability or a CIN-ready short-let is 12 to 24 months, not the six months on the agency PDF.

**Insider tip:** request a geometra-signed bill of quantities before the compromesso on anything marketed as turnkey renovated. Undocumented mezzanines and enclosed terraces are where surprise capex hides, and they only surface when someone measures the building against the cadastral plan.
