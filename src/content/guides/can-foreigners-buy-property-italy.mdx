## How Do Foreign Buyers Obtain a Codice Fiscale?

A codice fiscale is Italy's mandatory 16-character tax identifier that every foreign buyer on a rogito must hold before compromesso or bank account opening, with consulates issuing it free in 1 to 4 weeks and Italian counters often printing same-day certificates. Late codes delay more closings than notary availability.

| Codice Fiscale Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who needs it | Every foreign buyer named on the deed |
| Application location | Italian consulate or Agenzia delle Entrate office |
| Required documents | Passport, proof of birth date/place |
| Processing time | Same day in Italy, 2-4 weeks at consulates |
| Cost | Free |
| Validity | Permanent, no renewal needed |

- Joint buyers each need a separate codice fiscale; one code cannot cover two names.
- Proxy filings through an avvocato take 2 to 5 days after apostilled passport copies arrive.
- Banks refuse account opening without the code appearing in Agenzia databases.

**Checklist:** Apply 45 days before planned rogito, or use a proxy if the consulate queue exceeds your closing date.
