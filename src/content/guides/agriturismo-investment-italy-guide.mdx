## How does licensing work from farmhouse purchase to opening?

Licensing typically spans 4-8 weeks land due diligence, 3-9 months regional agriturismo classification, and 2-4 months health and fire inspections before opening guest rooms under active farming records on 3-5 hectares depending on region and production type in 2026 timelines. Non-resident buyers budget 10% to 15% closing stack plus IMU at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral.

Tuscany classifies agriturismo by type (A countryside, B farmhouse integrated, C educational). Umbria emphasizes olive and vineyard estates with strict kitchen rules. Puglia sees trulli conversions with pool-focused tourism; inspectors focus on septic, pool safety, and agricultural tie-in.

Operators must show minimum land (often 3 to 5 hectares depending on activity), equipment, and production records. A property with only ornamental olives fails classification.

**Insider tip:** A CIN or agriturismo licence does not transfer automatically; confirm it in writing before the caparra.
