Quick Answer: The compromesso is the legally binding preliminary contract in Italian real estate, requiring a deposit of 10% to 20% of the property value. ## What is the compromesso (contratto preliminare)?

The compromesso is the written preliminary contract that requires both parties to complete a defined rogito by a set date and typically collects 10% to 20% caparra confirmatoria on Italian sales from €200,000 to €3,000,000 before ownership transfers at the notary public for foreign purchasers closing in 2026.

| Element | Requirement | Typical value |
|---|---|---|
| Form | Written contratto preliminare | Bilingual for foreign buyers |
| Deposit | Caparra confirmatoria | 10%-20% of price |
| Timing | Rogito deadline | 60-120 days common |
| Due diligence window | Surveys and mortgage | 30-60 days |

- Proposta d'acquisto is non-binding; compromesso creates reciprocal obligation to sell and buy.
- Must list boundaries, permits, APE energy certificate, and existing tenancies in writing.
- **Red flag:** Verbal side deals on €600,000+ assets without written preliminary text.

## What is the difference between caparra confirmatoria and caparra penitenziale?

Caparra confirmatoria is the standard deposit that allows double-deposit penalties and Article 2932 specific performance, while caparra penitenziale is a fixed exit fee without court enforcement used in under 5% of 2026 residential deals above €300,000 in competitive city markets where foreign buyers need enforceable performance rights today.

| Feature | Confirmatoria | Penitenziale |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer default | Seller keeps 100% deposit | Seller keeps deposit only |
| Seller default | Buyer gets 200% deposit | Buyer gets 200% deposit |
| Specific performance | Yes, Article 2932 | No |
| Usage | 95%+ home sales | Rare flexible exits |

- International buyers should insist on confirmatoria before transferring 10% on €400,000+ city flats.
- **Red flag:** Penitenziale caparra offered on financed purchases without explicit buyer consent in writing.
- Discuss deposit type with independent avvocato, not agency templates alone.

## Is registration of the compromesso mandatory?

Registrazione is mandatory tax filing with Agenzia delle Entrate within 30 days of signing, costing €200 fixed registration tax plus 0.5% proportional tax on caparra confirmatoria and €16 stamp duty per four contract pages on typical €300,000 to €800,000 purchases closed by foreign buyers with F24 proof filed within 30 days.

| Tax / fee | Rate | Example on €50,000 deposit |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed registration | €200 flat | €200 |
| Proportional caparra | 0.5% | €250 |
| Stamp duty | €16 / 4 pages | €32-€64 |
| Notary filing | €300-€600 | €450 typical |

- Proportional tax credits against final rogito imposte; excess is not refunded.
- Late registration triggers fines and interest from Agenzia delle Entrate after day 30.
- Pay via F24 before submitting the signed preliminary contract.
- **Checklist:** Confirm proportional tax credit line appears in rogito imposte estimate from notary.

## How does transcrizione protect your deposit?

Transcrizione is voluntary Conservatoria registration by a notaio that publishes your preliminary claim so later seller mortgages or resale attempts rank behind your 10% to 20% deposit on deals from €350,000 to €2,000,000 across Milan, Rome, and coastal markets with seller encumbrance checks completed before caparra wires.

| Protection | Without transcrizione | With transcrizione |
|---|---|---|
| Double sale risk | Private contract only | Public block on title |
| Seller bankruptcy | Unsecured creditor | Privilegio speciale claim |
| Duration | N/A | 1 year from rogito date or 3 years from filing |

- Budget notary transcrizione fees inside €300 to €600 filing band plus counsel time.
- Align rogito calendar so protection does not expire before final deed signing.
- **Methodology:** Order seller insolvency and mortgage search before caparra on high-value stock.

## Which clausole sospensive belong in every compromesso?

Clausole sospensive are suspensive conditions that void the compromesso without penalty if mortgage approval, planning conformità, or agibilità checks fail within 30 to 60 days on financed foreign purchases above €250,000 with independent avvocato-drafted wording, bilingual annexes, and geometra sign-off attached before deposit release today under Civil Code rules.

| Clause | Purpose | Typical deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Mortgage | Loan approval | 45-60 days |
| Urbanistica | Planning conformity | 30-45 days |
| Agibilità | Habitability certificate | Before rogito |
| Condominium debt | Clean spese statement | 15 days |

- Seller must refund 100% deposit if condition fails without buyer fault.
- Include free-of-tenants delivery and cap on unpaid spese condominiali.
- **Red flag:** Seller refuses mortgage or conformità suspensive on a 15% deposit deal.

## What happens if either party defaults on the compromesso?

Default on caparra confirmatoria means the non-defaulting party may keep or double the deposit after a 15-day diffida ad adempiere, or sue for specific performance under Article 2932 on €300,000 to €2,000,000 preliminary contracts signed in 2026 with PEC diffida templates attached and counsel file opened locally.

| Defaulting party | Termination | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | Seller keeps 100% deposit | Seller sues for performance |
| Seller | Buyer receives 200% deposit | Buyer sues for transfer |

- Send diffida by PEC or raccomandata before activating penalties.
- Deposit remaining price with court if pursuing Article 2932 transfer order.
- **Case study:** Seller default on €450,000 Milan flat returned €90,000 double deposit on 15% caparra after diffida in 2025 files.
