## What heritage and STR rules apply in Umbria?

Assisi and Orvieto sit in UNESCO-sensitive zones, so exterior work needs Soprintendenza approval on an 8-12 week timeline. National CIN registration applies to any stay under 30 days, and enforcement fines run €500-8,000. The condominium's short-let vote should be attached before any centro ticket is underwritten at all.

| Rule | Timeline | Cost band |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Soprintendenza facade | 8-12 weeks | €3,000-8,000 consultancy |
| CIN registration | Before first guest | €200-800 |
| SCIA (where required) | 4-8 weeks | Commune dependent |
| Conformità interior | 6-10 weeks | Geometra led |

**Checklist:**

- Verify Assisi municipal STR caps with avvocato before deposit.
- Display CIN on all OTA listings before first booking.

## How do foreign buyers close in Umbria?

EU citizens buy freely with a codice fiscale and a notary rogito; non-EU buyers from reciprocity countries need avvocato review of cadastral consistency. Budget 10-15% closing on a second home including 9% registration tax on cadastral value, and read the visura catastale before the compromesso wire rather than after it.

| Step | Typical cost | Document |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Registration tax (second home) | 9% cadastral | Notaio calculation |
| Notary and legal | 1-2% | Rogito deed |
| Geometra survey | €800-2,500 | Rural and pool stock |
| Agency commission | 3-5% | Negotiable split |

A codice fiscale has to exist before the compromesso on any financed purchase.

### How do the region's markets differ from one another?

Umbria is landlocked, which removes the seasonal coastal pattern that dominates most Italian regional guides. Demand instead follows the university in Perugia, pilgrimage visitors in Assisi, and lifestyle purchasers in the smaller hill towns, and those 3 sources behave very differently across a year.

| Town | Demand driver | Letting pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Perugia | University and hospital | 12-month contracts, low vacancy |
| Assisi | Pilgrimage and heritage tourism | Year-round but uneven |
| Smaller hill towns | Lifestyle purchasers | Thin letting, longer holds |

- Heritage clearance constrains external works across the protected centres.
- Restoration capex, not purchase price, decides most rural Umbrian tickets.
- IMU applies at 0.86% to 1.06% of cadastral value on a second home.
