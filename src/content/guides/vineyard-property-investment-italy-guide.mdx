## What cantina licenses does a commercial winery require in Italy?

A commercial cantina in Italy requires Partita IVA, registered ATECO wine production codes, consorzio membership for DOCG or DOC labels, ASL sanitary approval for production areas, and national excise traceability before bottled sales. DOCG caps tie permitted bottle volume to declared hectares and historical yields. Registration tax on second homes typically costs 9% of cadastral value in 2026.

2. Cantina registration with appellation consortia (Chianti Classico, Barolo DOCG, etc.).
3. ASL sanitary approval for production and bottling areas.
4. Excise and traceability registration for bottled sales.
5. DOCG/DOC production caps tied to vineyard yields per hectare.
6. HACCP food safety if offering tastings with catering scale.
