## Self-collision, measured twice and fixed twice

This is the failure the playbook warns about and it happened both times.

**R2.** Twenty-seven sentence shapes were shared by three or more of the 25 pages,
concentrated in exactly the places R1 failed: the due diligence sentence (16
pages), the rental yield sentence (4), the cadastral-plan phrase (4), the
comparison-list construction (3) and the worked-example opening (3). Each was
reworded page by page. After the pass, 2 shapes remained and both were
all-stopword. The effect was visible in scores: `bologna` fell from 65 to 52 as
later pages landed and recovered afterwards.

**R3.** Six shapes shared by three or more of the 13 pages, again in the link
sentences. The "decision rule" heading appeared on all 13 pages and its FAQ
question on 8; both were rewritten individually. After the pass, zero.

**Across both waves.** Four shapes shared by three or more of the 38 pages, three
of them substantive; all fixed. Two all-stopword shapes remain.

## The registry, and what it is worth

Six figures verified during these waves were added to `.content-os/facts.json`
and three foreign claims to `.content-os/external-claims.json`. The rule followed
was: register a figure only if it was verified against a source during the wave;
reword a figure that was the writer's own illustration.

The spillover was measured rather than assumed. Adding three figures moved the
corpus mean from 19.86 to 19.97, which is 0.11 of a point across 272 files.
Registration is not a route to inflating the corpus.

Round durations and percentages that had been added to satisfy the fact-density
check (`20%`, `15%`, `40%`, `8 weeks`, `12 months`, `5 months`, `20 years`) were
reworded out rather than registered, because nothing had been checked about them.

## A scorer rule that punished correct writing

Provenance counted only `facts.json`, and foreign-jurisdiction figures cannot go
in that file by design. `compare/italy-vs-spain` cited Spain's €700,000 exemption,
sourced it in `external-claims.json`, which is the only file where it may be
sourced, and lost all ten provenance points for doing so.

`scoreProvenance` and `unregisteredSharedFigures` now consult `external-claims`
as well, keyed per file rather than per figure, so credit attaches only to the
documents a claim names. Measured: the Spain page 51 to 61, calibration unchanged
at 70.8, corpus mean 26.18 to 27.01. Documented in `docs/GEO-SCORING.md`.
