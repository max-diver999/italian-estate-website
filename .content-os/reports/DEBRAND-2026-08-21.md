## The mistake worth recording

The first attempt **substituted** `MORE Group` → `Italian Estate`. That was wrong
and was reverted. It turned 464 site-name mentions into 3,188 — minting 2,716
identical agent-voice stamps ("… desk", "… underwriting", "… files") across the
corpus. Those stamps are fabricated authority, and because the GEO uniqueness
rubric scores shingle novelty against the rest of the corpus, they measurably
*lowered* scores on 51 pages.

The brand had to be **removed**, not renamed. After the correct pass the site-name
count is 512 against 513 at HEAD — no new stamp was created.

`MORE Group <verb>s …` was advice wearing a brand costume, so it became the
imperative: "MORE Group models second-home IMU before net yield" → "Model
second-home IMU before net yield".

## Fabricated statistics found underneath

101 of the brand mentions were the grammatical subject of a claim about a private
dataset the site does not have: "tracked 16 cross-border Rome closings in 2025",
"our Milan desk screened 214 foreign enquiries", "MORE Group Case a 1 euro desk
screened 214 foreign enquiries in H1 2026". None is sourceable.

Per the standing rule — verify from real sources, reword so the claim does not
depend on unsourced precision, or remove — each was rewritten. Invented sample
sizes and closing counts did not survive. Cost and price bands did, restated as
guidance ("allow €15,000–€40,000"), because an estimate offered as advice is
honest in a way the same number presented as a measurement is not.

Two figures were kept because they are real and independently reported: 719,578
residential transactions in 2024 / ~766,756 in 2025, and ~8,700 foreign-buyer
rogiti at a ~€632,000 median. The fabricated attribution around them was dropped.

### Why the batch gate is red

| | Files | Total deficit | Mean | Worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| word count below baseline | 75 | 1,719 words | 23 words | 71 words |
| GEO below baseline | 50 | 138 points | 2.8 points | 7 points |

By collection: projects 58, guides 31, compare 27, areas 14, developers 1.

This is not a defect in the edit — it is the arithmetic of the edit. The corpus
lost 3,217 brand stamps and ~101 fabricated-statistic sentences, and the baselines
were recorded against a corpus that still contained them. A mean shortfall of 23
words is roughly one removed stamp sentence per page.

The two halves need different answers, and **neither should be applied without
Maxim's sign-off**:

- **Word count (75 files).** A floor derived from the previous corpus state
  measures the padding, not the quality. A ratchet that can never go down forbids
  cleanup by construction. The defensible fix is to re-derive the word-count
  baselines with `--force-metrics` and record *why* in this report — not to write
  1,719 words of filler back in.
- **GEO (50 files).** Different case. GEO is a quality score and should go up, not
  be re-baselined. The 2.8-point average drop is real: the removed sentences
  carried statistics, and the citability rubric rewards stat density. The honest
  fix is to restore that density with **sourced** figures — which is the same work
  as the fabricated-statistics wave proposed above, and should be done as that
  wave rather than patched here.

Recommendation: re-baseline word counts with a written justification, and fold the
GEO recovery into the next wave, where the fabricated numbers get replaced by
sourced ones rather than merely deleted.
