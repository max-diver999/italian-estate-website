# Where the next two weeks should go, and why not into the 48 files

Written after measuring, not estimating. Every number below is from Search Console, Bing Webmaster or Semrush on 6 September 2026.

## The finding: content is no longer the constraint

| | italian-estate.com | italiancitizenshipassistance.com | gate-away.com |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authority Score | **2** | 35 | 42 |
| Referring domains | **62** | 790 | 1,743 |

Those two competitors are the ones holding positions one to five on the golden visa, elective residence and digital nomad queries I spent this session writing against. **Their advantage is not their writing.** We have 12 to 28 times fewer referring domains.

Two more numbers say the same thing from the other side. Of 277 pages, **29 earn any impressions at all**. And inbound internal links barely move it: pages with 11 or more inbound links earn at 12%, pages with two or fewer earn at 6%. Linking helps and does not explain a corpus that is 88% invisible.

The trajectory confirms it. The site peaked on 4 and 5 July at 316 and 345 impressions a day **at average position 4.4 and 4.6**. It now runs 150 to 190 impressions a day at **position 22 to 39**. More pages, indexed, ranking worse. That is what an early-boost expiry looks like on a domain with no authority behind it.

## The evidence that the content itself is right

**Bing, last 30 days, against Google over the same period:**

| Engine | Impressions | Clicks | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google | 4,124 | 36 | 0.87% |
| **Bing** | 4,135 | **107** | **2.59%** |

Identical impressions. **Three times the clicks.** Across its full history Bing has served 716 distinct queries at 143 clicks from 1,794 impressions, an 8% CTR, on queries like *"what is scia when appling for italian cin"*, *"italy cgt 26 or 33"* and *"italian heir living in foreign contry competent tribunal"*.

Those are precisely the questions this corpus answers, and Bing rewards them because it weights domain authority less heavily. The content strategy works. It is waiting on authority for Google to agree.

## Why the 48 files are the wrong place for two weeks

They are not dead weight. Those 48 below-floor files **earn 217 impressions, 15% of the site total**, and include the single best-performing page on the site (`arezzo-centro-apartments`, 134 impressions).

Rewriting them raises quality scores. It cannot move a page from position 82 to position 8, because what holds it at 82 is a domain the algorithm does not trust yet. Two weeks of that work changes an internal metric and no external one.

## What to do instead, ranked by evidence

**1. Take Bing seriously as a channel, not a side effect.** It already delivers 3x the clicks. Concrete and available now: IndexNow submission on publish, checking which of the 248 Google-invisible pages Bing already ranks and building internal links toward those, and writing to the question-shaped long tail Bing is actually serving. This is cheap, it compounds, and it does not wait on anybody.

**2. Build one linkable asset and pitch it.** This session produced material no competitor has: the consular divergence on the digital nomad visa between London and New York, the Russia and Belarus suspension including dual nationals, the April 2026 law raising the 7% ceiling with its exact citation, and corrections to the OMI 2025 figures that most English guides still print wrong. That is the raw material for a piece the expat and property press cite. Referring domains are the only input that moves every page at once. The writing is mine; the outreach is not.

**3. Decide the portfolio question.** moregroup.estate sits at Authority Score 7 with 97 referring domains; italian-estate at 2 with 62; invest-gulf at 2 with 82. Roughly eleven sites are each carrying 60 to 100 domains. **Authority does not add across domains, it divides.** One property at Authority Score 25 would outrank eleven at 2 to 7 on every query any of them target. That is a business decision rather than a content one, and it is the largest lever on this list by a wide margin.

**4. Work the 29 pages that earn, not the 248 that do not.** Concentrate internal links, freshness and conversion on pages with demonstrated impressions.

## The honest summary

The last stretch of work fixed what was fixable in the text: corpus mean 32.9 to 45.5, five fabricated statistics removed, four new pages against real demand. What remains below the floor is real debt, and it is no longer the thing standing between this site and traffic.

At Authority Score 2 against competitors at 35 and 42, more words are not the answer. Bing is already proving the words are good enough.
